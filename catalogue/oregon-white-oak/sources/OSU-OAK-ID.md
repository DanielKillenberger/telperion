## Cited passages

### Oregon white oak, size

> - Size: Grows to 80' tall and 3' in diameter.
> Has a rounded crown when open-grown.

### Oregon white oak, leaves

> - Leaves:Simple,
> alternate, deciduous. Pinnately lobed
> with 7-9 rounded lobes;
> lobes often irregular. 3"-6" long and 2"-5" wide.

### Oregon white oak, bark

> - Bark: Grayish; may
> be shaggy or have shallow ridges and fissures.
