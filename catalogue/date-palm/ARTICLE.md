## Identity

Phoenix dactylifera belongs to the subfamily Coryphoideae and the tribe Phoeniceae, within the palm family Arecaceae. [M1](sources/M1.md)
The species grows on coasts in tropical and sub-tropical zones, in the Arabian deserts and in Africa, and Saudi Arabia is among the world's major producers of its fruit. [M1](sources/M1.md)
Beyond that placement, the record does not establish further identity detail such as native range boundaries or naming history; the extension sheet that would carry it has no passage captured here. [F1](sources/F1.md)

## Crown and habit

Palms in general, the family this species belongs to, are classed by habit as solitary, clustered or dioecious. [M1](sources/M1.md)

## Bark

This source studied trunk diameter and trunk circumference as diagnostic characters among cultivars, and found the trunk the least distinguishing of the character groups it compared, behind frond, leaflet, spine, fruit and seed. [M1](sources/M1.md)

## Leaves

Leaves are pinnate, borne on an armed petiole carrying narrow spines, with the blade split into multiple, single-folded, induplicate leaflets. [M1](sources/M1.md)
The source that measured this species' cultivars recorded each frond's color, length and width, the length of its pinnated and spined parts, the length, width and count of its pinnae, and the length and count of its spines. [M1](sources/M1.md)
The record does not state how many fronds a mature crown carries or how long each is retained before shedding; the extension sheet that would give that practical detail has no passage captured here. [F1](sources/F1.md)
