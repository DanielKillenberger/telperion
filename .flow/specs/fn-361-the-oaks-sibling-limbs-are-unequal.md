# The oak's sibling limbs are unequal

Child of fn-351 (P1's old trees; Phase 3). Host-written, 13 Nov 2026. Builds on the phase's top branch (`fn-360-a-runs-girth-eases-wherever-it-drops`).

## Problem

Measured (`.flow/evidence/fn-351-020-the-release-candidate/PHASE3-PROBES.md`, `PHASE3-TRIALS.md`): the Oregon white oak as the phase builds it loses its crown with age. Grower leaf on seed 1001: 757 m² at 120, 206 at 200, 41 at 240, 4 at 300; drawn at 300 it is a bare 7.8 m post. The cause is measured: tips are shed where their path from the root is long, in good light, and all sibling limbs are equal (the oak's limb row carries no `dominance`, where the ordinary tree, the spruce and the birch carry one), so every limb runs to the same length.

Trials with one preset value, `/space/ages/6/form/dominance` (the limb row; a lateral's share is `1 - d(1 - u^4)`), no code change, seed 1001, grower leaf m² at 120, 200, 240, 300:

| d | 120 | 200 | 240 | 300 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 757 | 206 | 41 | 4 |
| 0.25 | 726 | 505 | 213 | 43 |
| 0.5 | 599 | 570 | 509 | 344 |

At 0.5 on seeds 1007 and 1012 the leaf at 300 is 627 and 346 m². The host viewed the sheets (`phase3/T-B3.jpg`): at 200 a round full crown on heavy limbs, the best old oak drawn; at 300 living but thin; at 120 slightly thinner than d = 0 (drawn leaf 873 against 1,069 m²).

## The value and what it rests on (host)

`/space/ages/6/form/dominance = 0.5`, a listed estimate, not a sourced value. What supports it and what does not:

- **Chosen by:** the smallest of the tested values (0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75) at which the oak holds a living crown at 300 on three seeds. An oak that is dead by 300 contradicts the species; a source for the Oregon white oak's lifespan is not yet in the catalogue and is Phase 4's to add: until then this premise is itself unsourced, and the comment says so.
- **Checked against a measurement, which does not separate the doses:** the branch-diameter frequency exponent of Dassot, Fournier and Deleuze 2019 (Ann. For. Sci. 76:66; three Quercus petraea over 200 years; living wood over 7 cm sampled every 25 cm, 1-cm classes; -2.41, -2.37, -2.44; read in the paper's text by the host), taken the same way on the generated oak at 200 without the trunk, mean of three seeds: -2.20 at d = 0, -2.39 at 0.25, -2.34 at 0.5, -2.30 at 0.75 (two seeds). Some inequality sits nearer the measured range than none; the statistic cannot choose among 0.25 to 0.75 (R² per tree 0.45 to 0.85). `DIAMETER-SCALING.md`.
- **Not found:** any measured distribution of sibling branch sizes on an oak (`DOMINANCE-SOURCES.md`).

The value is a preset row like the other species' and changes the tree by degree; no code changes.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **R1.** The row in `crates/telperion-core/presets/oregon-white-oak.values`, placed with the limb row's form values, with a comment in the file's own style giving the estimate, who and when, what it rests on in two lines, and the two evidence files. Any generated mirror of the preset (catalogue pins, the package's tables) regenerated by its own tool.
- **R2.** Every digest pin and catalogue page that moves is listed with the tree it pins; only the Oregon white oak's move, and that is checked (the other six presets' pins unchanged).
- **R3.** The narrow suites pass: the preset, species-digest, space-preset and walk tests that read the oak.
- **R4.** The oak's panel of the scoreboard (fn-352's tool and protocol, read from `.flow/evidence/fn-352-the-scoreboard-a-fixed-panel-of-trees/`), drawn on this branch at the panel's seeds and ages, whole and leafless, with the panel's measures (height, crown, trunk, leaf, phytomers, peak memory, seconds where no pause fell inside the run), beside the base's sheet `base-2026-11-11`. A tree that cannot be drawn within the panel's memory is a row saying so with the amount it reached, not a gap.
- **R5.** The same measures for the other six presets are not redrawn here; say so.

## Stops

- A pin of another preset moving. A tree at 40 or 80 whose trunk, height or leaf differs from the phase's branch without the row by more than 25%: return with the numbers before drawing the rest.
- Any question about the value itself or a second row.
