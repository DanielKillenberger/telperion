# The slim field package grows every preset through the one build pipeline

## Conversation Evidence

> owner (2026-09-25), bug report from killenberger.com: "In telperion 0.1.3 the date palm grows through the main entry (`telperion.js`) but not through the slim field package (`telperion/field`). `growField("date-palm", seed)` throws `Field core: invalid input: family without a leaf plan` at every seed tried. Every other preset grows through the slim package."
> owner's report, acceptance: `growField("date-palm", s)` succeeds for seeds 1, 7, 1407 and 4242; its occupancy matches `engine.build(presetById("date-palm"), { field: true }).field.query` byte for byte; a test in the npm package grows every entry in `PRESETS` through `telperion/field`; a patch release 0.1.4 with the slim Wasm size noted.

## Goal & Context
<!-- scope: business -->

killenberger.com draws voxel trees from field queries and loads only the slim package to keep its download small, so it cannot offer the date palm that 0.1.3 shipped. The slim package keeps its own copy of the build chain, which fn-102 set out to remove: it refuses a family the leaf plan cannot describe, where the core pipeline falls back to a field read from placed leaves. The palm is the first shipped preset on that fallback. [paraphrase]

## Architecture & Data Models
<!-- scope: technical -->

- **What exists, checked 2026-09-25 on master (39348def).** `crates/telperion-field/src/grow.rs` runs `branching::generate`, `foliage::plan::plan` and `Field::planned`, and returns `InvalidInput("family without a leaf plan")` when the plan is `None` (`grow.rs:25`). It also skips `mesh::grow`'s rosette step (`clear_apical_twigs`) and `clothe_leaf_bases`, so for the palm even its skeleton differs from the main entry's. The core pipeline (`crates/telperion-core/src/pipeline/stage.rs`) builds the field from the plan where one exists (`planned_field`) and from placed leaves otherwise (`placed_field`). [checked]
- **Shape.** The slim crate asks the core pipeline for the field artifact only, as the main entry's Wasm binding does, and keeps no chain of its own. The field it returns carries the bounds `growField` exposes today. [inferred]
- **Host decision (2026-09-25).** The first build (the slim crate on `pipeline::build` with the `geometry` feature) grew `telperion-field.wasm` by 48% to 526,965 bytes, and the owner rejected it. The design instead: the slim crate stays on the one pipeline with a field-only request. The leaf plan describes a rosette, each frond, living and dead, as 2 to 4 descriptors along its arched rachis, on the placement's own spiral and frame, with radii from the leaflet extent and `count` its leaflets. Leaf placement and the `placed_field` fallback sit behind `geometry`, which the slim crate does not enable, and a family the plan cannot describe is refused there. The main entry's palm field then comes from the plan too, measured against the placed field as the share of cells that agree, with one still beside it. [decision]
- **Host decision (2026-09-25, second): the ribbon.** Capsules of the leaflet reach made the planned palm a round bush (81 % of cells agreeing, 2.8 times the placed foliage cells), so the plan gains a second primitive. A ribbon is a segment, a side vector (the half-width, square to the segment) and a thickness: the flat box the segment sweeps from `-side` to `side`, pushed out by the thickness along its normal. `Field::planned` tests a cell against it by separating axes, never as a round tube. Capsules stay as they were, and every other preset's field is byte-identical. Each frond, living and dead, is 3 chords along its arched rachis on the `rosette::fronds` spiral and frame. On each chord there is one ribbon per row of leaflets, rolled to that row's plane: one flat ribbon per chord reached 94.8 % and 1.34, short of the target. The ribbon is fitted to every corner of each leaflet's box, with the leaflet drawn on the frond's own stream exactly as placement draws it (`foliage/leaflet.rs`, shared with placement). Target on the quarter-metre grid at seeds 1, 7, 1407 and 4242: every placed leaflet vertex covered, at most 1.3 times the placed foliage cells, at least 95 % of cells agreeing. [decision]
- **Measured (2026-09-25).** The plan reports 1.24 to 1.28 times the placed foliage cells, and 95.1 to 95.4 % of cells agree (`tests/field_fronds.rs`). `telperion-field.wasm` is 347,167 bytes raw, 132,212 gzip and 108,609 brotli with its names stripped (0.1.3: 355,100 / 126,663 / 104,737). Unstripped it is 384,319 bytes. [checked]
- **Host decision (2026-09-25, third): the tapered ribbon, after the owner's verdict.** On the rectangular ribbons' still the owner said "right one looks much better": the placed field reads as pointed fronds with gaps between them, the planned one as blunt, merged fronds. Each ribbon's half-width was its chord's widest leaflet reach, so every frond was a rectangle. A ribbon's half-width now varies linearly from end to end, and its thickness does too. Its inner and outer edges, and its two faces, are each the line nearest the row that no leaflet corner crosses; the ribbon is the convex hull of its two end rectangles, tested cell to box. Chords: 4 a frond (3 to 6 and 8 tried). New target on the quarter-metre grid at the four seeds: at most 1.10 times the placed foliage cells, at least 97.5 % of cells agreeing. [decision]
- **Measured (2026-09-25, tapered, 4 chords).** 1.15 to 1.18 times the placed foliage cells, 95.3 to 95.5 % of cells agreeing, every leaflet vertex covered: both targets missed. Ceiling (`examples/palm_field_ceiling.rs`): the exact leaflets, each leaf's own oriented box tested cell to box, agree with the placed field on only 94.9 to 95.3 % of cells and hold 0.55 to 0.56 times its foliage cells. The placed field answers from each leaf's world-aligned box, which stands well past a diagonal leaflet. So 97.5 % agreement with the placed field is out of reach for any field that follows the leaflets, and the plan holds about 2.1 times the exact leaflets' cells. `telperion-field.wasm` is 355,200 bytes raw, 135,545 gzip and 110,995 brotli (0.1.3 plus 8.9 KB gzip and 6.3 KB brotli). The palm's plan takes about 13 ms native release, against 12.3 ms at 3 chords. [checked]
- **Host decision (2026-09-25, fourth): one oriented box per leaflet.** The owner rejected the ribbon look, and the ceiling measure showed that the placed field's world-aligned leaf boxes are not the truth. The rosette's plan is now one oriented box per leaflet, living and dead, from the shared leaflet code and the same replayed draws. It builds no instance or matrix buffer and nothing behind `geometry`. The box, `field/cuboid.rs`, replaces the ribbon: the rectangle a segment sweeps from `-side` to `side`, pushed out by its half-thickness along its normal, tested cell to box. A margin of 1 mm plus 0.5 % of the corner's reach covers packing. Targets, on the quarter-metre grid at the four seeds, are held to the real leaflet shapes (each placed leaf's own oriented box, `tests/oriented/mod.rs`): at least 99 % of cells agreeing, at most 1.05 times the leaflet cells, every leaflet vertex covered. [decision]
- **Measured (2026-09-25, boxes).** Against the leaflets, 99.72 to 99.73 % of cells agree, at 1.042 to 1.043 times their cells, with every vertex covered. For the record, against the placed field: 94.96 to 95.24 % agree, at 0.58 to 0.59 times its cells. The palm's plan plus field take 1.4 to 1.6 ms (native release), against 3.7 to 3.8 ms for placement, cull and the placed field. `telperion-field.wasm` is 344,798 bytes raw, 131,051 gzip and 107,758 brotli (0.1.3 plus 4.4 KB gzip and 3.0 KB brotli). Every other preset's field is byte-identical to master, and so is the placed palm crown. [checked]
- **Superseded.** The ribbon rounds above (rectangular, then tapered) are kept for the record; the box replaced them.
- **Unknown.** How much the placement and cull code adds to `telperion-field.wasm` (about 355 KB in 0.1.3); the implementer measures it and reports it in the PR. [unknown]
