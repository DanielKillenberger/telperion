# Trees that bloom before they leaf out

## Goal & Context

fn-234 (2026-10-09) made blossom read at distance: white petals now draw near white on both executors. What remains between a flowering cherry and its photograph is phenology: *Prunus avium* (and many fruit trees) bloom before or while their leaves emerge, so the photograph is a crown of flowers on bare twigs, while the engine draws a flowering tree with every leaf present, hiding about half its florets. A row for the leaves' presence while a tree flowers lets such species bloom on bare wood, by degree. Host (2026-10-09): after the release; the release's flowering trees (Laurelin, Telperion) bloom in full leaf.

## Requirements

- **R1:** one per-age row, the leaves' presence while flowering (1 neutral: every leaf, byte-identical; 0: bloom on bare wood), applied on both executors through the existing leaf presence, never a switch between ways of building.
- **R2:** a cherry-like test tree at 0.1 reads as the photograph's white mass at the whole-tree view, on stills viewed; organ coverage measured.
- **R3:** gate and npm test green.
