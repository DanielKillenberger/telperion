## Goal & Context
<!-- Goal & Context: 30% [user], 70% [checked] from the 2026-09-27 growth profile and the 2026-09-27 and 2026-10-02 measurements -->

Every growth step asks the crown how wide it is at a height, and each answer is computed from scratch. On master f9487810 (native, scratch sampler), `radius_at` takes 25.1% of oak seed 1's 51.3 ms growth, `radius_toward` 32.7% of beech seed 1's 103.2 ms, and `radius_at` 53.3% of birch seed 7's 268.4 ms, most of it inside the curtain search fn-174 covers. [checked]

The cost is the outline, not the lobes. Per query, the outline's `quadrant(p, s) = (1 - p^s)^(1/s)` takes about 51 ns; the lobes' single-octave Perlin noise about 27 ns (2026-09-27 investigation, re-measured 2026-10-02). `quadrant` depends on `p` and the shoulder alone. This spec tables `quadrant` once per shoulder value and makes every reader of the smooth outline read that table. The lobe noise stays exact. [checked]

## Architecture & Data Models
<!-- scope: technical -->

- **Readers today, checked 2026-09-27.** `local::planner::rejected` (admission), `Envelope::contains` (scaffold edges, attractor sampling), `Envelope::profile()` (shedding and crown exposure, a 129-point polyline sampled from `radius_at`), the curtain's `lower_surface` (`local/pendant.rs:260`), the GPU cull's `radius_at` and `quadrant` in `telperion-render/src/generation/place.wgsl:24-40` (the core's formula in WGSL, smooth radius only), and `waiting.rs`'s `tangent_height`, which needs the outline's slope. [checked]
- **One table per shoulder, shared by every view.** The direct build's inner envelope (`inner_envelope`, `branching.rs`) and the growth path's slice and planning envelopes change height, crown base and spread, never the shoulder (checked 2026-10-02). `p` is dimensionless, so one `quadrant` table serves the outer, inner, slice and planning views of a tree; a view supplies its own height, crown base, fullness and widest radius around the read. Preparation is per shoulder value, not per envelope, and the growth path prepares nothing per slice. [checked]
- **Ownership (Astra review, 2026-10-02).** The table lives under `envelope` as one immutable shoulder-and-table value, prepared once in the pipeline's input preparation and owned by the build. Views carry their envelope's dimensions and seed and a shared reference to it; validation, every view, foliage and the GPU executor read that one value and none prepares its own. No global cache, no per-envelope-and-seed cache. The hidden growth path keeps the same ownership. [inferred]
- **The table.** `quadrant` over `p` in `[0, 1]`, each half tabled over its distance to the end it leaves in octaves of equal cells, because the curve's slope is unbounded at the crown's ends (shoulder above 1) or at its widest height (below 1). A shoulder of 1 reads as its line. The first build's `envelope/shell/quadrant.rs` is this table: 2 to 3 KB per shoulder, reads at about 4 to 20 ns against 40 to 55 ns exact. [checked]
- **Lobes stay exact.** `radius_toward` on a lobed envelope is the tabled smooth radius times `1 + irregularity * clamp(noise::seeded(...))`, as on master. No height-and-bearing table, no lobe resolution limit, no refused `lobeScale`; the `lobe_scale` dial's window returns to the row's `[0.05, 1.0]`. [user decision 2026-10-02, from the 2026-09-27 investigation]
- **The bound (host, 2026-09-27; settled 2026-10-02).** The tabled outline differs from the exact `radius_at` by at most 1e-3 of the widest radius at every height, the whole budget now that the lobes are exact. The lobe term multiplies the smooth radius, so a lobed radius departs by at most `(1 + irregularity)` times that. Preparation keeps the first build's per-cell certificate (the chord of a monotone convex or concave cell from its end values and slope change, `envelope/shell/quadrant.rs`), its resource limits and its named refusal. A sweep of the shoulder dial cannot replace it: the rows accept shoulders outside the dial's `[0.4, 2.8]`, and sampling misses cell interiors (endpoint and midpoint sampling found 4.5e-4 where an interior scan found 5.5e-4; Astra review). The refused shoulders (about 0.13 and below) are where the certificate fails, not a separately coded cutoff. [checked]
- **The curtain's starting height.** `lower_surface` starts its climb at the smooth lower surface for the widest lobed radius, one exact `quadrant` call per search (`local/pendant.rs:269`), taken at the query radius less the bound in radius units (`1e-3 · max_radius · (1 + irregularity)`, subtracted before dividing by the widest lobed radius), clamped at zero and rounded down, so a start taken against the tabled outline cannot lie above its first crossing. A height lowered by the bound is not conservative (plan review, 2026-09-27). [inferred]
- **The supporting tangent stays exact.** `tangent_height` (`local/waiting.rs:63`) builds a supporting line from the analytic slope `quadrant_slope` and the radius at the same height. Both stay exact: a tabled radius would move the line without keeping it supporting, and a wake scheduled too late loses a birth the admission predicate can no longer recover (Astra review, 2026-10-02). [checked]
- **One outline, two executors.** The GPU cull reads the same `quadrant` table, uploaded with the cull configuration, in place of its WGSL copy of the formula. Sharing the table does not make the culls agree: the upload rounds samples to f32 (`data.rs:94`) and the shader forms the height coordinate in f32 (`place.wgsl:25`), and near a singular end coordinate rounding dominates (height 24, crown base 0.3, fullness 0.45, shoulder 2.8: `y = 24 − 2⁻²²` has radius 0.0028 of the widest exactly and 0 at f32 height 24). The GPU's departure is its own bound, stated separately from the CPU table's, with a permitted boundary disagreement. fn-125's plan layout carries the envelope and cull configuration (its R1), so it carries the table from the start, and its byte-identity base (R3) and speed base (R7) are taken after this change. [inferred]
- **What the first build carries that goes.** `envelope/shell/lobes.rs` (the height-and-bearing table, its `Grid::fit` and its derivative bounds on the seeded noise), the lobe half of `shell::holds` and of the bound budget, the `envelope lobes finer than the prepared shell` refusal, the `lobe_scale` dial cap at 0.15, `smooth_share` and `lobe_budget`, the seed-keyed six-entry `Shells` cache, the table built and discarded in validation (`shell.rs:91`) and the preparations in individual consumers. Kept: the quadrant table, `Envelope::widest`, one `quadrant_slope`, the dropped-triangle pins, the 128-corner envelope-dial test. [inferred]
- **Identity is not required.** The tree changes within the stated bound, under AGENTS.md "Generator evolution". [user]

## Edge Cases & Constraints

- Points outside the crown's height span answer zero, as `radius_at` does today. A zero-span envelope answers zero. [checked]
- Table memory and preparation time are recorded per shipped shoulder. [inferred]
- The table and its reads are repeatable native and Wasm under the pinned `Transcendental` implementation. [inferred]
- The Wasm artifacts stay within CI's size budget. [inferred]

## Acceptance Criteria

- **R1:** One prepared `quadrant` table per shoulder value serves every smooth-outline reader listed above, including validation, the GPU cull and every envelope view; a test counts one preparation per build. No reader evaluates `quadrant` per query, except `lower_surface`'s one starting height per search and `tangent_height`'s exact radius and slope. The lobe noise is evaluated exactly per query, and no table over bearing exists. No second copy of the formula remains in WGSL. [inferred]
- **R2:** The normalized table is tested against exact `quadrant` within the bound independently of seeds, across the shoulders the rows accept, at off-grid `p`, octave boundaries and both ends; a red/green test shows the certificate refusing a table that meets the bound at sampled points but not between them. View transformations (outer, inner, a growth-path slice and planning envelope) and the exact-lobe composition are tested over every catalogue and in-work preset at seeds 1 and 7. `lower_surface`'s starting height lies at or below the tabled outline's first crossing, including shallow-slope columns near the widest radius and shoulders below 1. `tangent_height`'s wake is no later than the first eligible slice against the new predicate, on lobed envelopes as well as smooth. Every lobed envelope whose shoulder prepares builds, including `lobeScale` 0.05 at irregularity 0.5. The table and a fixed query set are repeatable native and Wasm. [inferred]
- **R3:** `growth_profile` medians on base and candidate: oak growth at least 15% lower at seeds 1 and 7; beech and birch recorded against the first build's −21 to −26% and −60%; every other preset recorded; no preset more than 2% slower; preparation time and table memory recorded. All timings are paired against one named base. The hidden growth path at age 12 on the birch is no slower than master within the paired runs' noise, stated with the result. A miss on the oak stops with `NEEDS_HUMAN` and the profile. [inferred]
- **R4:** The suite's containment and curtain checks pass for every preset. The owner's visual verdict is recorded for the oak, beech and birch at seeds 1 and 7, on stills of at most four images per preset. [user]
- **R5:** Focused shader-against-reference tests compare per-candidate cull decisions at the crown's ends, octave boundaries and cull thresholds, within the GPU's stated bound and permitted boundary disagreement. The GPU and CPU culls agree on surviving leaf counts for the oak and spruce at seeds 1 and 7, and the harness's browser completed-frame medians for both are no slower within fn-125's 5% tolerance. [inferred]

## Decision Context

- **Lobe table dropped (owner, 2026-10-02).** The first build tabled the lobes over height and bearing: 0.8 to 5.8 MB per shell, 8.5 ms to prepare on the birch, fine lobes refused, the `lobe_scale` dial capped at 0.15, and the hidden growth path 70× slower on the birch because every yearly slice prepared new shells. It saved about 16 ns per query over the exact noise. The outline table keeps about 84 of the 104 ms the first build saved on a birch build (2026-09-27 investigation). [checked]
- **A cheaper exact formula was measured and rejected (2026-10-02).** One fast `quadrant` from short polynomials for ln, expm1 and exp, deterministic under plain IEEE arithmetic, is within 1e-7 at shoulders 0.45 to 5, ends included, but takes 39 ns against 51 ns exact for independent queries and 68 against 87 for dependent ones (reproduced by Astra). That rejects this implementation, which spends almost none of the 1e-3 budget; it does not prove a floor for every approximation. The table is taken as the strongest measured option, and a further approximation search is not a prerequisite. Probe source in the 2026-10-02 session scratchpad, `powprobe/`. [checked]
- **A cheaper shape was measured and rejected (2026-09-27).** Seeded harmonics in place of the lobe noise cost 30 to 55 ns against 27 and come out evenly spaced at 3 to 6 terms. A spline in radius over height cannot draw the vertical crown tip or the cusp (a parametric spline can, but radius at a height then needs an inversion per query), and is 0.3 to 14% off depending on whether its fit moves smoothly with the shoulder. A single power `1 - p^s` loses the rounded dome. A uniform table in `p` is 4.4% off in its last cell at shoulder 2.8 with 1,024 cells; a reparameterisation that smooths the ends costs a root, logarithm or inverse per query where the octave layout reads its graded coordinate from exponent bits. Caching by height is unmeasured and not added: the planner's bisection moves the height every probe (`planner.rs:146`). [checked except as noted; harmonic and spline timings not reproduced by Astra]
- **Astra review (2026-10-02, `ASTRA-REVIEW.md`).** Keep the design. Its findings are taken into this spec, except the call to restore the beech replay through Tune's shot selection (`replay.rs:194`): the owner dropped it on 2026-09-27, because its tape keys shots by rendered images and the runner is not meant to replay the same way.
- fn-91 task .12 rejected "prepared envelope admission bounds" on 1 to 4% browser gains and an unexplained 41,824 KiB RSS increase (`.flow/evidence/fn-91-.../envelope/REPORT.md`). That candidate kept the exact formula behind bounds. This spec replaces the formula for every reader, and R3 and R5 measure what fn-91 found missing. [checked]
- **Waits on fn-183 (2026-10-02).** A count by caller found 56 to 74% of the oak's, beech's and birch's crown queries come from the twig layer's per-stride outline check and its 40-step edge search (`.flow/evidence/fn-183-a-twigs-length-comes-from-its-room-in/QUERY-COUNTS.md`). fn-183 removes them by planning twigs from their room. This spec's rework is re-scoped against the queries that remain once fn-183's R4 counts are known. [checked]
