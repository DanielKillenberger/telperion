## Boundaries

General avoidance and repair of unrelated branch crossings remain fn4's responsibility. This work owns root transitions and related-branch surface anatomy, which fn4 currently excludes. Material bark appearance remains fn14; geometry required for collars and ridges belongs here. Full underground root/soil simulation and comprehensive bark microrelief are outside this first milestone. [inferred]

## Decision Context

The comparative benchmark supplies acceptance references. Local fixtures precede whole-tree integration so surface quality and cost can be evaluated without expanding every foliage element. Existing foliage supports the first connected base-to-leafy-shoot demonstration. [paraphrase]

Interactive Invigoration is a research comparator for lateral wood development; its representation is a candidate rather than an adopted architecture. [paraphrase]
