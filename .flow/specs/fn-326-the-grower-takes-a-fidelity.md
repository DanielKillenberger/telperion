## Acceptance Criteria

- **R1 (one input):** a family carries a fidelity in metres, the manifest's meaning, with every entry reading it through the family's parameters. Below 0 or not finite is refused by name. It sets the species the one cycle grows, for every executor; no entry grows another way. [paraphrase]
- **R2 (finest is the full cycle):** at fidelity 0 the species is the preset's unchanged, shown by a test that the grown structure is equal to the unscaled grow, on every preset. [paraphrase]
- **R3 (by degree, and it costs less):** an ordinary test on all eight presets counts phytomers simulated, retired ones included, at the manifest's fidelities. The count never rises as fidelity coarsens, and falls strictly on the oak, spruce and birch. [paraphrase]
- **R4 (values, not code):** each species' coarsening is rows in its preset's value file, judged as the other rows are. A preset without rows is dormant: fidelity changes nothing. No species name or branch in the code. [paraphrase]
- **R5 (the numbers):** the oak, spruce and birch at preset age on held-out seeds at each manifest fidelity: the six R4 medians and one-thread seconds, in a results file. [inferred]
- **R6 (nothing else moves):** the gate is green; each shipped artifact stays within its budget. At fidelity 0, the preset's existing outputs are unchanged. [inferred]
