## Goal & Context

Telperion 0.2.0 grows every tree through the one botanical cycle, shows it growing instead of making anyone wait for it, draws it fast from a hero tree to a forest, grows large old trees efficiently, and looks right to the owner. 0.1.4's look is not the target: the owner holds 0.2.0's botanical growth to be the better way and its trees to look bad today. This rewrite of 9 Nov 2026 replaces the goal of 1 Nov, on the owner's word, after its route was used up.

What the first fifteen children showed. From 1 to 9 Nov the run tried to make the full cycle cheap by approximating it and holding the approximation to the cycle's own statistics within 5%. Four approximations failed that bar: a cut with density standing in (fn-323), fewer and longer phytomers untuned (fn-324), values derived from the species' tables (fn-334), and a stand-in read from a twig's own state (fn-335: 0.2% on the spruce at best, 5% on the oak and birch, 10 to 11% on the beech, and only with per-species averages). Rows tuned per species held 3 to 5% at 5 to 10 times the speed, and the owner ruled them out twice. Meanwhile nothing was done on why 0.2.0's trees look bad, on drawing fast, or on fixing old trees, and no coarse tree was ever shown to the owner.

So this goal drops the search for a cheap copy of the cycle. A tree is the full cycle's tree. Speed comes from never blocking on it, from showing its real younger states while it grows, from the engine's own cost, from growing many trees across cores, and from drawing less of a tree that is far away.

Measured, at this rewrite's base: the full cycle in Wasm on one core takes 3,584 ms for the oak, 11,947 for the spruce, 1,230 for the birch, 258 for the beech, 1,000 for the ordinary, 17 for the palm, 9,836 for Telperion and 8,845 for Laurelin (fn-329). 0.1.4 in a browser takes 233 to 260 ms for the oak, 203 to 212 for the spruce and 239 to 304 for the birch, in a 107,663-byte Brotli download (AUDIT-2026-10-26.md). The full oak grows to 300 in 210 s and 1.95 GiB on one thread (fn-330, fn-333), with its trunk bent near level and its crown 8 to 16 m off its base by 200 (fn-332). The cycle spends 75% in its year loop and 24% in its last settle, with no pass above 26%; allocation and copies are about 20% and transcendental math about 15% (REVIEW-FABLE-2026-11-09.md).
