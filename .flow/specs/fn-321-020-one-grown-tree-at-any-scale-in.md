## Goal & Context

Telperion 0.2.0 grows a tree by one law run three ways. Wood that can outgrow a cell of the tree's own light is grown bud by bud, and its form is earned from light, vigour and the wood's own mechanics. The leafy shoots inside a cell are counted as cohorts moved by the same law. A twig is built from its key when something looks at it. The tree grows on the CPU for every consumer, a tree at any age is the grower's own state, and each species is held to real trees and to the owner's eye.

This replaces the goal as rewritten on 9 Nov, on the owner's word of 10 Nov, after seven surveyors read the generator. Their findings and the reasoning are `RETHINK-2026-11-10.md` in this goal's evidence. In short: the cost is twigs nobody sees (97.5% of the phytomers grown by 200 are shed by then, and at a whole-tree view every fine internode is under a pixel); the look is prescribed by timers and angles set by eye, with light only pruning; a second grower on the device is four times the size of the first and cannot pass age 95; and every earlier route to speed kept the twig-by-twig cycle as the truth and tried to copy it. That cycle is not a truth worth copying: the spruce grows 50 to 70 times a real spruce's shoots a year and the beech carries a fifth of a real beech's leaf.

At this rewrite's base, the full cycle takes 1.1 to 2.3 s for the oak at 40 on one thread and 77 s to 300 on four; its trunk bends near level by 200 with the crown 11.9 m off its base (fn-330, fn-336, fn-337 BEFORE.md).

## Acceptance Criteria

Every threshold tagged [inferred] is the host's number, listed again under Open Questions for the owner to change. Measurements run through `telperion-heavy`, on the integration branch's head, and state their commit and seeds.

- **R1 (one law, three ways):** every tree is grown by the species' one law. An axis that can outgrow a cell of the light lattice is grown bud by bud. The leafy shoots that cannot are carried as cohorts on the wood that bears them: whole numbers by age, drawn by lineage key, moved each year by the same species tables over a shoot's own life with the same hazards read at the cell. Where the boundary falls follows from the cell's size and the species' own shoot lengths, with no row and no value per species, and it moves by degree with both. An ordinary test holds that the buds grown in a year follow the structural axes and not the leafy shoots. [paraphrase]
- **R2 (form is earned):** on the structural axes, vigour follows light across the whole tree and sets how much a shoot grows; a lateral rises in rank by its vigour and not by a timer; a limb holds its lean while its wood's gravitropic performance allows and bends where it does not; height slows as the path from the root lengthens. Each rule is read from a branch's present state, rests on published botany checked against its text, and takes measured traits for values. Every architectural row of a real species names its source or is listed as an estimate with its reason; no row is fitted to hit a target, and the count of estimates per preset is reported and is lower than at this rewrite's base. [paraphrase]
- **R3 (held to real trees):** for each real species at its preset age, and the oak also at 120 and 300, on at least twelve seeds: leaf area index, new shoots a year, trunk diameter against height, height against age, and the crown's width against its height when open-grown each sit inside the published range for the species, the source's text checked. A measure with no source for a species is reported and does not count for or against. [inferred]
- **R4 (the owner's eye):** the owner's written verdict in this spec that each real species reads as itself beside its reference photographs at whole, mid and close views; the oak at 120, 200 and 300; a twig at 5 cm; and a tree growing in view. Only a verdict written here counts, and a rejection fails it. Agents prepare the sheets as retained JPEGs. [paraphrase]
- **R5 (grown fast):** native, one thread: the oak at 40 in at most 200 ms and to 300 in at most 30 s, with cost per simulated year rising by at most 10% a decade once its height growth is under 1% a decade. One thousand distinct oaks at 40 in at most 20 s across this machine's cores. In a browser on the CPU, every species 0.1.4 has is at or under 0.1.4's time for a tree at its preset age. - **R6 (a twig on demand):** a leafy shoot built from its key is the same shoot in every year it lives and under any view. - **R7 (one grower, one geometry, a year is a state):** growth runs on the CPU, native and Wasm, for every entry; the device draws and grows nothing, and the device grower with the code that holds the CPU to it is deleted. Wood is laid once. The tree at year y is the grower's state at year y: the same whatever age was asked for, and the same grown in one call or paused on the way. ## Decision Context

The owner accepted the approach on 10 Nov 2026 with what the host said it reopens: the leafy shoots stop being stored state and become a function of the structure; the device grower is deleted; every preset is re-derived on sourced traits. [paraphrase]

Why the boundary is the light cell: the lattice is the scale at which parts of a tree tell each other apart in this model, since everything in a cell deposits into and reads from one number. A subtree that cannot leave a cell meets the rest of the tree only through totals. fn-334's reach rule drew the same line and held with no row. [inferred]

Why cohorts are the model and not a copy of it: botany counts organs this way (GreenLab's populations, the pipe model, leaf area index), `closed_form` already computes the expectation from the same tables, and fn-335 measured a fine lateral growing 0.43 to 1.21 of its tables' potential over its own life with shedding, not light, removing what is lost. The cycle is not the reference: no criterion asks a tree to match it. [paraphrase]

The device still has work: drawing, levels of detail, and it may build the twigs in view, which are independent of one another. It does not grow structure: a tree's sums run up and down its hierarchy, which took about 14,000 small dispatches a tree, a fixed 76 to 80 ms, and a 2 GiB buffer that refuses the oak at 95. If the first proof shows the structure alone is still costly, this is reopened. [paraphrase]

Known rules and their sources, each to be read again by the child that builds it: gravitropic performance and the lean a stem can hold (Alméras and Fournier 2009; fn-337 and fn-338's notes hold the expressions read so far); the hydraulic limit on height (Ryan and Yoder 1997, which gives no functional form, so the form is this model's and is stated as such); vigour shared by light at forks (Borchert and Honda, as in Palubicki et al. 2009); branch modules judged visually alike at 99 and at 1,204 a tree (Makowski et al. 2019); what reads as real (Polasek et al. 2021: fork angles and parent-to-child ratios first). [paraphrase]

What could sink it, from RETHINK-2026-11-10.md: a cohort moved by expected rates can drift where growth feeds back (October's hand-written closures ran 14 to 17% over on the oak); the earned rules may not draw a good oak; it rewrites the grower's core and every baseline; a built twig must agree with its cohort. The three proofs are aimed at these. [paraphrase]

Earlier forms of this goal: `git show cc0587d68` (1 Nov) and `git show 21a5931cd` (9 Nov). fn-338 is open at its first planned stop; its note feeds the second proof, and the run retires it when that proof's child is minted. [paraphrase]
