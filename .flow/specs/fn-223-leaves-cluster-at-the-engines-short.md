# Leaves cluster at the engine's short shoots

## Goal & Context

The silver birch (fn-213) reached its second wrong-path stop on Astra's traits (j) and (h) on sheet 5: 'repeated feather-like leafy sprays attached to long exposed axes' and foliage that follows thin sprays. The cause, found by the run (SHEET-5.md): leaves are seated evenly along every thin shoot, one every 12 mm, so a long hanging shoot reads as a two-sided feather; a birch carries most of its leaves two or three together on short shoots, in clumps along the strand. The owner's round-10 verdict (2026-10-08) named the same thing: leaves "small and numerous to appear like they just fill the twigs length". The engine grows short shoots as their own physiological ages, but nothing seats a leaf cluster at them; the canopy's `/canopy/shortShoot*` rows scatter clusters by their own placement, not at the engine's short shoots (claim from the run, to be checked against the code in the task before building). Host decision (2026-10-07): a capability, not more values. Many broadleaves (beech, oak, apple) bear leaves this way.

## Requirements

- **R1 (which shoots, which tip):** a short shoot is any engine axis whose physiological age states a cluster (R2's row above 0); the selection is the PA, never a radius, a node kind or a run end. Its cluster sits at the axis's last kept phytomer, the shoot's own tip: one cluster per axis, owned by that axis whichever side of the partition seam its nodes are dressed on, never at a run boundary or a seam. A shoot whose terminal phytomers were shed or merged takes its last kept node as its tip; a shoot with no kept phytomer bears no cluster. Conversion carries what this needs (the axis's PA or its cluster row, and which node is the tip) into the pipeline's tree on the CPU path, and the device's records carry the same on the device path (fn-212's handoff); stated in the task before code, checked against `pipeline/space.rs::convert` and the device records.
- **R2 (rows):** two per-PA engine rows: `cluster_leaves` (whole leaves per cluster, 0 to 8, default 0) and `cluster_spread` (radians, 0 to π/2, default 0). At `cluster_leaves = 0` nothing changes, so every species is byte-identical. Above 0, the axis's evenly spaced leaves are replaced by the cluster: a short shoot bears its leaves only at its tip. Long shoots keep today's spacing row; there is no separate thinning row (deletion before addition). Values outside the bounds are refused by name.
- **R3 (one mechanism):** the canopy's `/canopy/shortShoot*` rows are checked against the code: if R1 covers what they do for engine species, they are deleted with their tests and every preset row that sets them is migrated to R2's rows, shown by stills; otherwise kept with a stated reason recorded in the spec.
- **R4 (identity):** each cluster leaf's draws are keyed by its shoot's tip node lineage and its index in the cluster, through the lineage key registry (fn-212's station-identity rule), so both executors seat the same leaves.
- **R5 (proved):** positive fixtures: an engine tree with clustering on, showing exactly one cluster of `cluster_leaves` at each selected tip, none on long shoots, and the replaced leaves gone; CPU and device paths equal in cluster count and within fn-125's screen in position across the partition seam; neutral rows byte-identical to fn-212's landed baseline (stills and hashes); invalid rows refused; a shoot with no kept phytomer bears none; the foliage instance budget still refuses over-budget trees by name; cost of clustering measured on a tree with it on.
- **R6:** gate and npm test green.

## Boundaries

Leaf seating only. Depends on fn-198 (with fn-212 merged). The birch's values are fn-213's.
