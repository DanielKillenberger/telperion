# Leaves hang on twigs

## Goal & Context

The owner (2026-10-09), looking at the release branch's trees: "I think major problem in terms of looks now is the lack of twigs. All the leaves seem to come from branches and they are directly attached." On a real tree most leaves sit on the current and last few years' thin shoots and spurs, each on its own petiole, so between a visible branch and its leaves there is a fine layer of twigs. Why the engine's trees show none is unknown; candidates, each to be checked against the code: the engine grows no twig-order shoots for these species (the finest axes are too few or too short); the twigs are grown but not drawn at the harness's views (wood below the curve surfacing's screen error, or below a radius cull, dropped while leaves stay); the twigs are drawn but too thin or too dark to read; the petiole (`/element/connectorLength`) is too short or not drawn; or leaves are seated on thicker wood than they should be (fn-223's clusters, the station spacing on slender wood). The owner then narrowed it: "i was talking about telperion and laurelin". The Two Trees are the beech's architecture scaled 2.4-2.5× by fn-217's S1, which scales positions, lengths and radii once while leaves keep their natural size; a leading hypothesis to check first is that S1 makes every twig 2.4-2.5× as long and thick, so beside natural-size leaves the twigs read as branches and the leaves as attached straight to them (a real big tree carries more orders of ordinary-size twigs, not giant twigs). Host (2026-10-09): release blocker.

## Requirements

- **R1 (find it first):** for Laurelin and Telperion first, against their natural base the beech at the same age (scale 1), then every other woody shipped species (beech, oak, spruce, birch, ordinary, Laurelin, Telperion; the date palm's fronds are placements on an unbranched stem by design, so for it only the frond attachment at the apex is checked) at 40 and 80 (seeds 1, 7, 42), at the harness's whole, mid and close views: how many leaves sit on wood of each radius class and each age (current-year, 1-3 years, older); how much of that thin wood is grown, and how much is drawn at each view (the curve surfacing's selection, any radius cull); stills of a branch tip close up with the twig layer highlighted; reference photographs of real shoots of the same species beside them (for the ordinary tree, labelled botanical analogues; for Laurelin and Telperion, beech, their natural base). The cause named and checked against the code; if it is a value a species set, the fix is values; if it is how wood is drawn or where leaves are seated, the design goes to the host before code.
- **R2 (fixed):** on every woody species, leaves read as borne on twigs and petioles at the harness's mid and close views (the palm's fronds stay as they are), and the crown's fine texture at the whole view reads as twiggy where the species is; every parameter by degree; both executors; frame rates held to fn-246's.
- **R3 (proved):** the same stills before and after, viewed; the owner's verdict; gate and npm test green.
