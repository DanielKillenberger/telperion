# The spruce's distance fixture and the lichen's own integral

## Goal & Context
<!-- scope: business -->

fn-71 made every bark term leave by the box integral of its own shape, and the owner accepted the result in the harness. Two named pieces did not fit inside it, and both are stated in that spec's report rather than papered over. [paraphrase]

The first is a measurement, not a defect. fn-71's R1 asks the 4x distance series to hold on the oak and the spruce; the shipped series asserts the oak alone, because the spruce's bare crown throws twig shadows across its trunk at every strip the fixed fixture can frame. Plain wood with no relief at all reads 6.2/255 there at 2x and 13.6 at 4x, and the p95 is a shadow edge's 26 to 42, so the fixture measures its own shadows rather than the bark's filtering. The spruce's bark is held meanwhile by the grazing fixture at 2.70. [paraphrase]

The second is the last term still leaving by a fade. fn-71 removed the amplitude fade from the relief, the plates, the dashes and the grain; the lichen keeps its fade from two pixels across to one, and a relief-free beech shows that what remains of the beech's loss from 4x outward is the lichen patches and lenticel rims integrated as one edge, which stands for a disc only while the box is narrower than the spot. ## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- scope: both -->

- **R1:** The 4x distance series asserts the spruce beside the oak, from a pose or mask clear of its own crown's shadows, and holds at or under 3.0/255 mean with its receipt beside the oak's. [paraphrase] Errors: a fixture that cannot separate the bark from the crown's shadow reports that rather than a number, and the spruce stays on the grazing fixture until it can.
- **R2:** The lichen leaves by the box integral of a disc over the footprint with a window that grows with the box, its amplitude fade gone, and the footprint sweep stays flat within 0.03 between adjacent factors on the beech base, the birch bark and the birch hero notch. [inferred] Errors: a residue the integral cannot reach is stated with its size in code values rather than faded away.
- **R3:** The frame cost is recorded beside fn-71's round four (oak 8.9, birch 7.0, beech 14.6 ms p50) and any rise is attributed by term. [inferred] Errors: none beyond the record; the owner sets any bound.
