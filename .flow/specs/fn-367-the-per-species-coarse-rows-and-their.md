# The per-species coarse rows and their dial are deleted

Child of fn-351 (P6; Phase 5). Host-written, 14 Nov 2026. Builds on `fn-364-a-tip-extends-by-what-its-balance-leaves`. Readings: `.flow/evidence/fn-351-020-the-release-candidate/PHASE5-CODE-ANSWERS.md` (answer 7), `PHASE5-SURVEY-2026-11-13.md` ("The coarse rows, and who reads them").

## Problem

P6: "no preset carries a `/space/coarse` row". The rows (`/space/coarse/{first, factor, leaf, bud, hazard, shedding}` on the oak, the spruce and the birch) are fitted per species (fn-325, fn-326, fn-328: medians within 5% on tuning seeds), which the owner ruled out on 3 Nov ("This shouldn't be per species") and 9 Nov ("i don't think we want hand tuned rules"). The plan kept them until counted foliage could replace them, on the premise that they make the website fast. Checked on 14 Nov: every shipped preset states `fidelity` 0, and no code in `src/`, `harness/`, `tests/browser/` or `scripts/` sets another value (the harness panel offers the row as a hand control). Nothing shipped reads them. They are deleted now, not replaced.

## The design (host)

- Delete the rows from the three presets with their comments, `Species::coarsened` and `species/coarse.rs` (`Coarsening`, `COARSEST`), their reads in `GrowInput::of`, `Family` validation, the blend, the preset parser and writer, and `pipeline/fidelity_tests.rs`.
- **The `/fidelity` parameter goes with them.** With no coarse row it changes no tree, and a parameter that changes nothing is a dead setting (`docs/principles.md`: every parameter changes the tree by degree). It leaves the family, the wire, `parameters.generated.ts` and `presets.generated.ts` (by their own generators), `docs/parameters.md`, the catalogue and the harness panel. If a general dial returns, it returns with its law. R1 decides whether anything other than the coarse rows reads `family.fidelity` (the grep shows the word in the render crate and the foliage levels; those may be another meaning of the word): if something else reads the parameter and changes a tree or a drawing by it, return with the reader before removing the parameter, and delete only the rows.
- The pin `every_preset_grows_the_tree_it_grew_at_both_ends_of_the_dial` keeps its fine end under a name that says what it holds; its coarse end is deleted with the thing it pinned.
- No shipped tree changes: every preset's digest at `fidelity` 0 is equal to the bit before and after.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **R1.** The reading first, in the evidence: every reader of `Coarsening`, `coarsened`, `COARSEST` and of `family.fidelity`, each marked "coarse rows only" or "reads the parameter for something else", with what the something else is.
- **R2.** The deletion as designed; `cargo build` of the workspace and of the Wasm crate clean, no dead import, no orphaned test helper.
- **R3.** Every shipped preset's digest unchanged (the existing pin's fine end, and `every_preset_grows_the_same_tree_again_and_on_any_cores` by name).
- **R4.** `catalogue_identity`, `parameter_reference` and the package's parameter tests updated by their own procedures and green; `presets.generated.ts` and `parameters.generated.ts` regenerated by their generators, never by hand.
- **R5.** Lines deleted and the Wasm module's raw bytes before and after, recorded.
- **R6.** Narrow tests while building; the two sub-second guards before finishing.

## Stops

- A reader of the parameter that is not the coarse rows (see the design).
- A shipped tree whose digest moves.
- A walk or blend test that used the dial as its only way to reach some state: return with the test, do not delete it.

## Boundaries

The rows, their code, the parameter. No change to the grower's laws, the pipeline's stages or any other preset value.

## Rigor

Opus builds. Heavy runs through `telperion-heavy` with `TELPERION_HEAVY_RESERVE=8` in the background, checked by the clock; the yield watcher may freeze them. Friction in this spec's FRICTION.md as it happens.
