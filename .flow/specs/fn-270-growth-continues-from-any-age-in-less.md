# Growth continues from any age, in less memory, with leaves that unfold

## Goal & Context

The owner (2026-10-13): Telperion's 4,209 MB device peak "seems like way to much peak memory"; "If we could just ask for a tree at an age and pass that tree back into the generator as input to grow from => the consumer can render the previous one while the next one is generated"; "alright add this to release, but make sure to test/fail early if something doesn't hold up. Maybe council first?"; "Can we have growing leaves? because right now having instantly large leaves makes it much less convincing." Growth is the product (AGENTS.md, "Botanical growth is the product").

Council round 8 (`.flow/evidence/fn-270-growth-continues-from-any-age-in-less/council/astra-8.md`, with its brief `round8.md`) agrees on the shape and found the premise that must be checked first: the growth law reads the requested final age through `presence::Windows` (`presence.rs:47`, used in shedding at `grow/cycle/passes.rs:56`), so a tree grown to 40 inside an 82-year request differs from a 40-year request. Host decision (2026-10-13): the release keeps today's trees by making the final age an explicit **horizon** given at `begin`; growth past the horizon is refused by name; a growth law that needs no horizon (open-ended growth) is a later spec, a model change for the owner's verdict.

## Requirements

- **R1 (premises, first, on the CPU; stop on failure):** (a) every read of the final age in the cycle, the settle and dormancy is listed against code; (b) under the horizon, the tree at year a of a horizon-H run equals 0->a->H's prefix, and a walk in the horizon has no pop; (c) the continuable retirement rule (below) keeps the kept tree byte-equal to today's on every preset at seeds 1, 7, 42, with the live set per cycle measured; (d) 0->H equals 0->a->H byte for byte, a at 10, 40 and H-1, with a settle between, including zero-year advances, repeated settles, and splits around waking, shedding and PA transitions. Stop on any same-executor mismatch, snapshot mutation, or a live-set **peak** rise over 25%. Host ruling (2026-10-13, after task 1): the stop reads the peak, because the peak is what the arena is sized by; mid-life cycles hold up to +91% more (spruce, cycle 24) and that is measured input to R4 lever 4, not a stop. "No pop" in (b) means the horizon adds none of its own (it held: PREMISES.md); shedding's whole-year steps are today's law and R5a smooths them within the year.
- **R2 (API):** `begin(params, seed, horizon) -> GrowthState`, `grow_to(&mut state, age)` (absolute age, idempotent, refused by name past the horizon, asynchronous on the device), `settle(&state, at) -> Tree` (R2a) (an independent snapshot; never mutates the state). The state holds only what the next cycle reads (lineage, axes, pencils, live phytomers and units, sleeping and woken buds with their future events, memories, records and frozen sums, the census, an explicit boundary flag for whether the last cycle's layout and light are complete); light lattice, job counts, verdict columns and hierarchy partitions are dropped between calls and rebuilt, with their rebuild cost measured.
- **R2a (committed age and presentation age):** growth commits whole cycles only; the state's committed age is an integer. `settle(&state, at)` takes a presentation age `at` in (committed - 1, committed]: it draws the committed tree with this year's leaves and new shoots expanded by their curves at the fraction (R5); presentation never feeds back into growth and never mutates the state. Tests in R1: fractional settles leave the state byte-equal; `settle(at = committed)` equals today's whole-year tree; the presentation at a fraction just past a whole year joins the previous year's tree with no pop: new organs start at their curve's first value, and old organs are drawn as year committed−1's (R5a's blend at fraction zero).
- **R2a' (fractional requests):** `grow_to(a)` commits cycles up to ceil(a); a request with committed - 1 < a <= committed is already satisfied and commits nothing; only a <= committed - 1 counts as backward and is refused (R2b). The sequence 40 -> 40.25 -> 40.5 -> 41 commits cycle 41 once (at 40.25) and later requests do nothing; `settle(at)` for each of them draws the presentation at that fraction; the final tree equals 0 -> 41 grown in one call. Tested in R1 with settles between calls.
- **R2b (failure contract):** non-finite ages, ages before the committed age, and ages past the horizon are refused by name before any mutation; a runtime failure (budget, capacity, a device fault flag, device loss) invalidates the state permanently and every later call refuses it by name; the consumer's last snapshot stays valid. Forced-failure tests for each.
- **R2c (admission while drawing):** continuation admission charges the persistent state, the drawn snapshot, settle scratch and every allocation overlap together, and never releases the drawn tree to make room (today's `MakeRoom` path, `resident/budget.rs:98-103`, `web/generation.rs:84-88`, does not apply to continuation); when the overlap cannot fit, the growth step is refused by name and the previous tree stays drawn. Test: a lit preset advances year by year with its previous tree drawn throughout.
- **R3 (retirement without the final age):** a closed subtree is retired once it has no living or potentially waking bud, its waking shares are final, and its fade has reached nothing at the state's age; still-fading subtrees stay eligible later (a pending set or expiry schedule, not a permanent Kept); everything later settles read is kept.
- **R4 (memory, one lever at a time, a labelled census after each; drop a lever that frees under 10% of the previous peak):** (1) persistent state, settle inputs and transient scratch separated, scratch shared where lifetimes do not overlap; (2) the worst-ratio-over-all-presets sizing replaced by parameter-derived estimates calibrated on the presets (no species branch); (3) axes compacted for every parameter set, the records' axis readers moved to a small archive; (4) capacity growth on continuation with overlap charged. Targets (investigation, unmeasured): Telperion and Laurelin build peak at most 2,000 MB, spruce 1,200, oak 650, birch 450, beech 250, ordinary 250, palm 32; the oak at 80 builds under the 4 GiB default. Standalone and continuation peaks reported apart. The guard and the full-card run hold.
- **R5 (leaves and shoots unfold):** time within the year: `grow_to` and `settle` accept fractional ages; each leaf's size follows a sourced expansion curve over its first weeks and each new shoot extends along a sourced elongation curve over its season, by degree; whole-year ages draw exactly today's tree. Fail-early test before the device work: one tree at several fractional ages within one season, viewed, no pop.
- **R5a (everything between years moves smoothly; host, after task 1: the presentation at fraction f in (committed−1, committed] blends every organ that exists in both years from its year committed−1 drawing to its year committed drawing, keyed by axis identity and place on the axis (position, scale, girth, sag), and a subtree gone in year committed fades from its year committed−1 drawing to nothing across the same fraction; the state keeps the previous year's drawn attributes for this, their memory counted in R4's continuation peak. At f→0 the tree is year committed−1's to the bit in its old organs (task 1 measured 94,909 of the oak's year-40 organs moved or gone by 41, which this removes); owner, 2026-10-13: "can we at some point interpolate between years if that were necessary?"):** the presentation at a fraction also blends each axis's girth and sag between the previous committed year and this one (the state keeps last year's girth and bend for that), and a subtree shed this year fades or drops over a short stated window instead of vanishing; a woken bud's shoot starts at zero length like any new shoot. Whole years draw exactly today's tree; presentation never feeds back into growth.
- **R6 (the harness shows growth):** the harness grows the tree year by year (and within the year) as the product, drawing the last settled tree while the next stretch grows; frame time while growing measured; `?growth=1` no longer hidden.
- **R7 (consumers and proof):** fn-257's field query and the Wasm/WebGPU entry take the state; a browser without a device gets a named error; per-preset grow-per-year and settle cost reported (the settle named as the next lever); gate and npm test green; a recording of Telperion and the oak growing in, viewed; the owner's verdict.
