# Growth continues from any age, in less memory, with leaves that unfold

## Goal & Context

The owner (2026-10-13): Telperion's 4,209 MB device peak "seems like way to much peak memory"; "If we could just ask for a tree at an age and pass that tree back into the generator as input to grow from => the consumer can render the previous one while the next one is generated"; "alright add this to release, but make sure to test/fail early if something doesn't hold up. Maybe council first?"; "Can we have growing leaves? because right now having instantly large leaves makes it much less convincing." Growth is the product (AGENTS.md, "Botanical growth is the product").

Council round 8 (`.flow/evidence/fn-270-growth-continues-from-any-age-in-less/council/astra-8.md`, with its brief `round8.md`) agrees on the shape and found the premise that must be checked first: the growth law reads the requested final age through `presence::Windows` (`presence.rs:47`, used in shedding at `grow/cycle/passes.rs:56`), so a tree grown to 40 inside an 82-year request differs from a 40-year request. Host decision (2026-10-13): the release keeps today's trees by making the final age an explicit **horizon** given at `begin`; growth past the horizon is refused by name; a growth law that needs no horizon (open-ended growth) is a later spec, a model change for the owner's verdict. Owner (2026-10-13), on the horizon: "is there a way to not have to fix maximum age? but i guess yea that works."

## Requirements

- **R1 (premises, first, on the CPU; stop on failure):** (a) every read of the final age in the cycle, the settle and dormancy is listed against code; (b) under the horizon, the tree at year a of a horizon-H run equals 0->a->H's prefix, and a walk in the horizon has no pop; (c) the continuable retirement rule (below) keeps the kept tree byte-equal to today's on every preset at seeds 1, 7, 42, with the live set per cycle measured; (d) 0->H equals 0->a->H byte for byte, a at 10, 40 and H-1, with a settle between, including zero-year advances, repeated settles, and splits around waking, shedding and PA transitions. Stop on any same-executor mismatch, snapshot mutation, or a live-set **peak** rise over 25%. Host ruling (2026-10-13, after task 1): the stop reads the peak, because the peak is what the arena is sized by; mid-life cycles hold up to +91% more (spruce, cycle 24) and that is measured input to R4 lever 4, not a stop. "No pop" in (b) means the horizon adds none of its own (it held: PREMISES.md); shedding's whole-year steps are today's law and R5a smooths them within the year.
- **R2 (API):** `begin(params, seed, horizon) -> GrowthState`, `grow_to(&mut state, age)` (absolute age, idempotent, refused by name past the horizon, asynchronous on the device), `settle(&state, at) -> Tree` (R2a) (an independent snapshot; never mutates the state). The state holds only what the next cycle reads (lineage, axes, pencils, live phytomers and units, sleeping and woken buds with their future events, memories, records and frozen sums, the census, an explicit boundary flag for whether the last cycle's layout and light are complete); light lattice, job counts, verdict columns and hierarchy partitions are dropped between calls and rebuilt, with their rebuild cost measured. Host ruling after task 2 (2026-10-13): the hierarchy's shape, counts and current lists are state, not scratch (the next step and retirement read them; zeroing them changed 7 of 8 young presets), so they stay resident; a from-scratch rebuild would save nothing at the growth peak and is not built. Retirement's six per-cycle columns inside `ret` move out under R4 lever 1 (task 3). The continuation peak with the previous tree drawn (Telperion 5,603 MiB, Laurelin 5,152) is over the 4 GiB default and is task 3's to bring under the R4 targets. The state owns its parameters (no borrow, no `unsafe`), settled when task 5 needs an owned state.
- **R2a (committed age and presentation age):** growth commits whole cycles only; the state's committed age is an integer. `settle(&state, at)` takes a presentation age `at` in (committed - 1, committed]: it draws the committed tree with this year's leaves and new shoots expanded by their curves at the fraction (R5); presentation never feeds back into growth and never mutates the state. Tests in R1: fractional settles leave the state byte-equal; `settle(at = committed)` equals today's whole-year tree; the presentation at a fraction just past a whole year joins the previous year's tree with no pop: new organs start at their curve's first value, and old organs are drawn as year committed−1's (R5a's blend at fraction zero).
- **R2a' (fractional requests):** `grow_to(a)` commits cycles up to ceil(a); a request with committed - 1 < a <= committed is already satisfied and commits nothing; only a <= committed - 1 counts as backward and is refused (R2b). The sequence 40 -> 40.25 -> 40.5 -> 41 commits cycle 41 once (at 40.25) and later requests do nothing; `settle(at)` for each of them draws the presentation at that fraction; the final tree equals 0 -> 41 grown in one call. Tested in R1 with settles between calls.
- **R2b (failure contract):** non-finite ages, ages before the committed age, and ages past the horizon are refused by name before any mutation; a runtime failure (budget, capacity, a device fault flag, device loss) invalidates the state permanently and every later call refuses it by name; the consumer's last snapshot stays valid. Forced-failure tests for each.
- **R2c (admission while drawing):** continuation admission charges the persistent state, the drawn snapshot, settle scratch and every allocation overlap together, and never releases the drawn tree to make room (today's `MakeRoom` path, `resident/budget.rs:98-103`, `web/generation.rs:84-88`, does not apply to continuation); when the overlap cannot fit, the growth step is refused by name and the previous tree stays drawn. Test: a lit preset advances year by year with its previous tree drawn throughout.
- **R3 (retirement without the final age):** a closed subtree is retired once it has no living or potentially waking bud, its waking shares are final, and its fade has reached nothing at the state's age; still-fading subtrees stay eligible later (a pending set or expiry schedule, not a permanent Kept); everything later settles read is kept.
- **R4 (memory, one lever at a time, a labelled census after each; drop a lever that frees under 10% of the previous peak):** (1) persistent state, settle inputs and transient scratch separated, scratch shared where lifetimes do not overlap; (2) the worst-ratio-over-all-presets sizing replaced by parameter-derived estimates calibrated on the presets (no species branch); (3) axes compacted for every parameter set, the records' axis readers moved to a small archive; (4) capacity growth on continuation with overlap charged. Targets (investigation, unmeasured): Telperion and Laurelin build peak at most 2,000 MB, spruce 1,200, oak 650, birch 450, beech 250, ordinary 250, palm 32; the oak at 80 builds under the 4 GiB default. Standalone and continuation peaks reported apart. The guard and the full-card run hold. Host rulings after task 3's lever 1 (2026-10-13, MEMORY.md): **lever 2 merges into lever 4** — tables start from the closed-form hint at a lower calibrated margin and grow by observed counts between cycles (geometric growth, moves counted, old+new overlap charged to the guard, hashes unchanged across moves); no per-parameter ratio fit, because unseen points of the species space must neither fail nor need a table, and a per-seed 2.3x spread defeats any fixed ratio; warm build time per preset re-measured against fn-237 R0a. **Lever 3's archive freezes each retired subtree's radius contribution at retirement** (option a): girth laid down does not depend on later settle-time values, the change is horizon-free and continuable; it changes bytes, so the radius shift is measured per preset and stills compared beside today's, and the owner sees it in R7's verdict. Lever-1 compactor/cycle-scratch sharing (at most 3.7%) is dropped under the 10% rule. Task 3 then found the records' other reader, the tree's leaf mean (fn-197 takes it over the whole tree as grown, retired phytomers included); host ruling: **a retired subtree's two leaf-mean sums are frozen at retirement beside its radius** (option B), added where its root stands in the reduction order, so fn-197's whole-tree mean keeps its meaning; refined (host, same day): the values are frozen at the subtree's **close** (nothing in it lives or sleeps), carried up in the device's existing bottom-up living sweep in the same order as the CPU, so no new sweeps and exact CPU/device equality stay; dead wood does not ripen after it closes; base and leaf mean factored out as MEMORY.md states; records and inside axes then go. The Two Trees' 2,000 MB is not reachable by R4's levers (measured floor about 2,745 MB, the settle's scratch about 1,050 MB and retired-row records 376): the release accepts the measured floor provided the standalone peak falls at least 30% and the grown-on peak is under the 4 GiB default; the settle's scratch is a later spec. After task 3 (host): the oak at 80 is refused only because the starting sizes overshoot it 3-4x; the starting allocation is capped to what the budget leaves (margin lowered only when the hint-sized tables would not fit), and growth by observed counts does the rest, so no shipped preset's time moves; the oak at 80 then builds under the default, measured. The Wasm CPU peak (Telperion 3,114 MB, under wasm32's 4 GiB) gets no CPU lever in this release; it is reported in R7.
- **R5 (leaves and shoots unfold):** time within the year: `grow_to` and `settle` accept fractional ages; each leaf's size follows a sourced expansion curve over its first weeks and each new shoot extends along a sourced elongation curve over its season, by degree; whole-year ages draw exactly today's tree. Fail-early test before the device work: one tree at several fractional ages within one season, viewed, no pop.
- **R5b (palm fronds have an age; owner 2026-10-13: "option 1 seems good"):** a rosette crown's fronds are aged by their place in the spiral; a new sourced row, fronds per year (FAO 2002: 10 to 26 new leaves a year), sets how many are this year's, and those unfold over the year by the PA's expansion curve; the crown is keyed to the fronds, not redrawn from the moving apex, so it holds still within the year and joins both years with no pop; by degree, dormant where a species has no rosette.
- **R5c (the seasonal year; owner 2026-10-13, on seeing the oak unfold still in last year's leaf: "they don't look like they're growing from buds though?", then "i mean that sounds awesome.. possible without too much cost?"):** the presentation's year runs from one whole age's tree, in full leaf as today, to the next: autumn, when leaves past their lifespan colour and fall; winter, bare of them; budburst, when the year's shoots and every leaf of the year (on old wood too) grow from nothing; full leaf at the next whole age. Whether a leaf falls comes from the leaf-retention rows the species already has (a leaf kept under a year falls, evergreen needles and the palm's fronds stay and only the new ones unfold), never a deciduous switch. New sourced rows per species, checked against source text: senescence onset and duration, leaf-fall duration, budburst day relative to the year's clock, and the autumn colour (a renderer input added once, read by every species alike). Host rulings (2026-10-14, after the worker's sourcing pass): (1) which leaves fall is a new sourced per-PA row, the **leaf's lifespan** in years, apart from how many years wood bears leaves (`leafYears`, unchanged, so whole ages stay today's trees); a leaf older than its lifespan at autumn falls, by degree (spruce's 6-7 years drops its oldest cohort, the deciduous trees' under one year drop every leaf, short-shoot clusters included, and grow every one from its bud at budburst). (2) A whole age sits at full leaf: budburst + elongation + expansion; sourced calendar dates convert from that anchor. (3) A row with no source for the species takes a declared stand-in, never blocking: the nearest congener's sourced value (as the oak's Q. alba and Q. robur already do) or, failing that, the centroid rule the ordinary tree uses, each marked stand-in in the catalogue; autumn colour, which no source measures, is the species' sourced verbal colour ("yellow", "russet", "copper") mapped through one fixed named-colour table, marked named, not measured. (4) The palm: fronds per year 18, the FAO 2002 midpoint, stated as such; frond expansion the derived 139 days (Zhen et al. 2015's elongation over FAO's mean frond), marked derived, low confidence. Whole ages draw exactly today's tree; growth is unchanged; the cost per frame is measured and must stay within the presentation's (no new device sweeps, no memory beyond R5a's).
- **R5a (everything between years moves smoothly; host, after task 1: the presentation at fraction f in (committed−1, committed] blends every organ that exists in both years from its year committed−1 drawing to its year committed drawing, keyed by axis identity and place on the axis (position, scale, girth, sag), and a subtree gone in year committed fades from its year committed−1 drawing to nothing across the same fraction; the state keeps the previous year's drawn attributes for this, their memory counted in R4's continuation peak. At f→0 the tree is year committed−1's to the bit in its old organs (task 1 measured 94,909 of the oak's year-40 organs moved or gone by 41, which this removes); owner, 2026-10-13: "can we at some point interpolate between years if that were necessary?"):** the presentation at a fraction also blends each axis's girth and sag between the previous committed year and this one (the state keeps last year's girth and bend for that), and a subtree shed this year fades or drops over a short stated window instead of vanishing; a woken bud's shoot starts at zero length like any new shoot. Whole years draw exactly today's tree; presentation never feeds back into growth.
- **R6 (the harness shows growth):** the harness grows the tree year by year (and within the year) as the product, drawing the last settled tree while the next stretch grows; frame time while growing measured; `?growth=1` no longer hidden.
- **R7 (consumers and proof):** fn-257's field query and the Wasm/WebGPU entry take the state; a browser without a device gets a named error; per-preset grow-per-year and settle cost reported (the settle named as the next lever); gate and npm test green; a recording of Telperion and the oak growing in, viewed; the owner's verdict.
