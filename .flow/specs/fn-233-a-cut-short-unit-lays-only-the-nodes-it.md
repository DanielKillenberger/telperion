# A cut-short unit lays only the nodes it lived to lay

## Goal & Context

fn-231 measured that a partial growth unit lays every node of its unit, each scaled down by its share, so a shoot cut short carries its full node count crowded into its short length. fn-231 fixed this only for the partial units a fractional lifespan makes, because those make the walk jump. The same crowding remains in a failed unit's lived part (fn-199, `lived_extent`), a relay's rest of the cycle from other causes, and a woken bud's partial first unit. Botanically a shoot that stops early has laid fewer nodes, not all of them small. After the release (host, 2026-10-08); it changes every shipped species with viability below 1 or sleeping buds.

## Requirements

- **R1:** fn-231's per-zone rule (each zone lays its first share s of its nodes, the last as a making draw that grows in) applied to every remaining partial unit, on both executors, with the predictor counting at share.
- **R2:** every shipped species measured and re-judged on strips before and after, viewed; re-pinned with that evidence.
- **R3:** gate and npm test green.

## Boundaries

Depends on fn-231. Not a release item.
