## Goal & Context
<!-- scope: business -->
<!-- Goal & Context: 25% [user], 55% [paraphrase], 20% [inferred] -->

The owner wants a template-building pipeline that takes a taxon from literature to a species packet, growth curves and a preset, with TypeSafe judging the evidence along the way. The deliverable is a runbook and a toolset: the owner points an agent at them and the agent produces the new species template. Which model the agent runs on is the owner's choice for that run, and the pipeline must give the same template whichever it is, so a cheap model can do it and a strong one gains nothing by reading. [paraphrase] Today that path is the prose handoff in the species onboarding doc. A research agent reads sources, a profile author fills the packet with values and a hand-written confidence, the fn30 script composes curves from tables transcribed into its own comments, and a 15 percent tolerance plus the owner's eye judge the result. Every step that reads is done by whichever model happens to be driving, which is how a site-classification criterion came to stand behind the oak's ten-year height. [paraphrase]

The design principle that makes the driver's model irrelevant is that the pipeline leaves nothing for the driver to judge. Every reading step is a Jev question through the caller and tools fn-57 builds, every arithmetic step is code, and every choice that belongs to a person lives in one of two places: the manifest a person authors before the run, or a decision the run files and a person resolves. The driver runs commands in order and passes file paths along. [paraphrase] Firecrawl, installed as the CLI, is the first fetch adapter: search and the research index propose candidate sources, scrape returns markdown beside the raw HTML, and parse turns a fetched PDF into markdown. [paraphrase]

The pilot behind fn-57 showed the pieces work on real text. Twelve of twelve sentences classified by kind, the site criterion pushed out at 0.99, six true claims verified and the misuse routed to a person at confidence 0.26, each call under a second. What the pipeline adds is the sequencing, the packet with its provenance as the typed output, a data-quality gate before any fit, the curve fit from selected points, and the proof that a small model driving it produces the same outputs as a large one. [paraphrase] The gate answers the owner's costliest lesson: fn30 spent its rounds composing and rendering a Garry oak growth curve for which no open-grown age-indexed height existed under 30 years, and found out at the end. Judged on the same evidence on 2026-09-16, Jev scored that field proxy-only at 0.87, named the wrong growing condition as the gap at 0.98, and put the chance a fit should proceed at 0.05; a control field with open-grown points at every age scored sufficient at 1.00. [paraphrase] A plan review by the owner's standing reviewer on 2026-09-16 returned nine findings; the ones the host agreed with are folded into the sections below. [paraphrase]

## Architecture & Data Models
<!-- scope: technical -->

- **The manifest is the human boundary, drafted by the agent.** The manifest names the taxon and its context, the admitted sources with their checksums, the proxy species and site class where a proxy is used, the composition method for each curve, the value tables for described traits, the engineering parameters with their rationale, and the versions of every question set and tool. The agent drafts it from the discovery and data-quality outputs, with every proposed entry carrying the ranking or gap judgment behind it, and files it as a manifest-proposed decision. A person admits it, edited or not, and only the admitted manifest is read by the later stages, which never edit it. A choice that is not in the admitted manifest is not the driver's to make; it becomes a decision. [paraphrase]
- **Stages as commands with schemas.** The pipeline is a fixed sequence of commands. Each takes the manifest and the paths of earlier outputs, writes its complete output atomically to a fixed path under the species' evidence directory, and records the ledger references of every judgment it used. Every artifact has a named schema with a version and a canonical serialization, sorted keys and fixed number formatting, so two runs can be compared byte for byte. A stage's idempotence key covers its input checksums, the manifest checksum, the question-set versions, the model name and the tool versions; when the key matches the recorded one the stage does nothing. [paraphrase]
- **The stages.** Source discovery, fetch and checksum, candidate extraction, screen, data-quality gate, select, packet verification, curve composition and fit, onboarding gate, generation and measurement, then the report. Discovery proposes sources for a person to admit into the manifest. Fetch and parse use the adapter. Screen, select and the citation check come from fn-57. The four question sets this spec adds, source ranking per field, data sufficiency per field, described-level scoring and semantic obligation checks, are versioned data with labelled cases, run through fn-57's caller and ledger. [paraphrase]
- **The data-quality gate runs before anything is fitted or rendered.** For every evidence field the manifest requires, code lays out the screened candidates with their kind, condition, taxon and ages beside the requirement, and counts the measured points and the required ages they cover. Jev scores sufficiency on four described levels, none, proxy only, partial, sufficient, and names the dominant gap: no age-indexed points, wrong condition, wrong taxon, or an uncovered age range. A field below the bar the manifest sets files a data-insufficient decision naming the gap and the sources tried, and no select, fit or render runs for that field until a person admits a proxy, adds sources or lowers the bar. The gap also drives a second, targeted discovery pass. The stop is code on the level against the bar; the pilot's yes-or-no question on whether to proceed was unreliable on the control case at 0.41 and is not the rule. [paraphrase]
- **The packet keeps its closed records, provenance goes beside them.** The pipeline fills the existing profile, references, species and specimens records the onboarding protocol defines and adds no keys to them. A provenance sidecar keyed by JSON Pointer carries, for every filled value, its source ID, the copied span or table row, and the ledger reference of the judgment that selected it. A field with no admissible candidate is recorded as unavailable with the reason, never estimated. [paraphrase]
- **Evidence fields and engineering parameters are separate.** Evidence fields are what the literature can supply, height and trunk diameter at ages, crown width and base, organ dimensions, and described traits such as crown habit and branch droop. Engineering parameters, recruitment, shoot step, shedding, foliage placement and their kin, are authored by a person in the manifest with a rationale per value, or produced by the fit where the fit owns them, and are marked as engineering in the sidecar with that origin. The pipeline never produces an engineering value by any other route. A number reaches an evidence field by one of four routes and Jev never writes one: a span copied from a source, a fit over such spans, a described level, or a reference transfer. On the described route Jev scores the level over levels a person wrote, code proposes candidate values from the value table the manifest names, generation renders and measures each candidate, Jev may rank the candidates from their measured comparison, and the value that ships is the one code proposed and a render measured, as the owner's TypeSafe rule of 2026-09-16 requires. Code selects it by a fixed rule: the candidate whose measured trait lands inside the level's stated range and nearest its midpoint, ties to the first in table order; when none lands inside, the trait is a decision, never a nearest miss. The level, the sentence, the candidates, the measurements, the ranking and the ledger reference sit in the sidecar; probabilities live only in the ledger entry behind that reference. [paraphrase]
- **Reference transfer drafts a value from the templates already tuned.** For a dial the literature cannot fill directly, the state is the new species' screened sentences beside the screened sentences and the shipped values of every template already onboarded, and two questions run per dial in one parallel request: a Choice over the tuned templates plus none for the nearest reference on that trait, and a Score over levels written as botanical situations for the relation to it, clearly smaller, about the same, clearly larger and their neighbours. Code computes the candidate from the reference's shipped value and the level, interpolating when two references bracket the new species, and the candidate then enters the same render-and-measure step as the described route; the value that ships is still one code proposed and a render measured. Each onboarded species adds one anchor, so the route starts weak with the oak and the spruce as the only references and improves per species. The route may also fill the drafted manifest's engineering entries with the nearest reference's values and that reference as the rationale, which a person admits or edits like any manifest entry; the pipeline still produces no engineering value past the admitted manifest. The reference, the level, the computed candidate, the measurements and the ledger reference sit in the sidecar. [paraphrase]
- **Decisions stop the stage and a person resolves them elsewhere.** A decision has a stable ID derived from species, stage, kind, field and age, a typed payload per kind, and a status. A ledger reference is derived from the state checksum, the question-set version, the question ID and the model name or version, so identical requests share one identity across runs and drivers, and the ledger entry behind it carries the probabilities, timestamps and usage. The run appends to the decision list atomically and deduplicates by ID on rerun. Resolutions live in a separate file a person writes, keyed by decision ID and carrying the decision's input checksums; a resolution whose checksums no longer match the reissued decision is void and the decision reopens. A stage whose inputs carry an open decision it depends on does not run. [paraphrase]
- **The artifacts.** Thirteen named artifacts, each with a schema at version 1: manifest, sources, candidates, the four packet records the protocol already defines, provenance sidecar, decisions, resolutions, report, ledger and command log. Command names and paths are the plan's to fix; the spec fixes the artifact set and its schemas. [inferred]
- **The pipeline is built once; a species is a spec that runs it.** Under the owner's one-species-per-spec rule, each new species gets its own spec holding one authored manifest, one run of this pipeline, the decisions a person resolved, the capability gaps that became dependency specs, and the checklist the owner ticks on the stills. The species spec restates none of the method; this spec and the onboarding doc are the method. [paraphrase]
- **The run halts at a gap; the gap loop resumes it.** When the onboarding gate names a capability the generator lacks, or a described trait's rendered candidates all miss their level, the run stops with that decision and does nothing about the gap. Proposing fixes, judging them, routing the decision, minting the fix as its own spec and resuming the run from the halted stage are fn-63-gap-loop-options-escalation-and-resume's work, which depends on this spec. [paraphrase]
- **The driver's runbook.** One line per stage, in the shape of the bridged worker's pointer prompt, with no instruction to read, summarize or decide. The driver's command log is an output of the run, so the swap test can check that only runbook commands ran. [inferred]

## API Contracts
<!-- scope: technical -->

The decision list is the one new shape. Each entry is one of the kinds below; the fields shown per kind are the contract.

```json
{
  "id": "oregon-white-oak/curve-fit/tolerance-miss/dbh_m/26.7",
  "species": "oregon-white-oak",
  "stage": "curve-fit",
  "kind": "tolerance-miss",
  "status": "open",
  "inputs_sha256": {"manifest": "…", "points": "…"},
  "ledger": ["…"],
  "payload": {"field": "dbh_m", "age_years": 26.7, "measured": 0.266, "reference": 0.064, "error_percent": 316.6, "sources": ["E1", "G1"]},
  "options": ["accept-composed-reference", "anchor-on-stein-mature-open-grown", "reject"],
  "note": "The composed young-age diameter reference is a stand-grown table plus an open-grown integration from a 7 cm anchor."
}
```

Kinds and their payloads: tolerance-miss carries field, age, measured, reference, error percent and sources; proxy-choice carries field, the proxy taxon and the source; rights-unclear carries the source and the rights text found; claim-contradicted and claim-unsupported carry the claim, the source section and the relation distribution; coverage-gap carries the source, the expected and found row counts; missing-curve carries the dimension and the ages with no admissible point; data-insufficient carries the field, the sufficiency level with its distribution, the dominant gap, the measured points found with their conditions and taxa, the required ages left uncovered, and the sources tried; level-miss carries the trait, the chosen level, the level's target range, and every candidate value with its measurement; unavailable-source carries the source and the fetch error; manifest-proposed carries the draft manifest's checksum, each proposed source with its ranking judgment, and each proposed proxy or composition choice with the data-quality gap that motivated it; onboarding-gate carries which gate is unresolved, capability, registration or audited seeds; visual-unassessed carries the still paths. A resolution is `{"id": "…", "inputs_sha256": {"manifest": "…", "points": "…"}, "option": "…", "by": "…", "at": "…", "note": "…"}`, and it binds only while its checksums match the decision's. Every stage's output carries the stage name, the schema version, the manifest checksum, the input paths with their checksums, the question-set versions, the model name, the tool versions and the ledger references it consumed. [inferred]

## Edge Cases & Constraints
<!-- scope: technical -->

- **Source bytes stay outside the repository.** The evidence directory holds checksums, extracted candidate sentences and rows, the packet, the sidecar, the decisions and the ledger. The fetch adapter's cache directory is ignored. [paraphrase]
- **Raw and transformed artifacts get separate checksums.** The fetch stage records the final URL, the content type, the checksum of the raw response, and the checksum of the markdown it judged, because the markdown is a transformation that can differ between runs. A PDF is fetched to a local file and its bytes checksummed before parse. [paraphrase]
- **The fetch adapter's own extraction is not used.** The scrape query and schema options put a number-writing model in the path. The pipeline uses markdown, raw HTML, parse and search only. [inferred]
- **A flattened table is a coverage gap against a known count.** The manifest records the expected row count for each admitted table, from a person's reading of the source. When the parsed table yields fewer age-indexed rows, the stage files a coverage-gap decision rather than selecting from a broken table. [paraphrase]
- **An unauthenticated or failed fetch files unavailable-source** with the adapter's error and stops the stage. Nothing is fetched by another route in its place. [paraphrase]
- **Structural obligations are checked in code, semantic ones by Jev.** Provenance present on every filled value, an asset hash on every inspected image, a non-empty rights field, a resolvable source ID, these are code checks. Whether a reference's observation describes a page rather than an inspected image, and whether a stated value is a measurement or an invention, are Jev questions with labelled cases. [paraphrase]
- **No number reaches the packet from a model.** The selection stage copies spans; the fit stage computes; the level route maps through a table a person authored; the reference route computes from a tuned template's shipped value and a scored relation. A judgment answers with option keys and probabilities only. [paraphrase]
- **Judgments are stable in their answer, not in their probabilities.** Four identical calls on the O1 sentence on 2026-09-16 returned the same option every time with the kind confidence fixed at 0.99, while the condition confidence moved between 0.80 and 0.88 and the anchor probability between 0.05 and 0.06. Comparisons across runs therefore use the chosen option, the level, the decision and the copied value, never the probabilities, which stay in the ledger. [paraphrase]
- **Generation stays deterministic and offline.** The generation stage runs the existing measurement example and the headless renderer with the key unset, as fn-57's guard requires. The swap test and every stage that reaches the network are excluded from the workspace test commands. [paraphrase]

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- scope: both -->

- **R1:** The pipeline is a fixed sequence of named commands, each reading the manifest and earlier outputs at fixed paths and writing one complete artifact atomically to a fixed path, under a named schema with a version and a canonical serialization. A stage reruns only when its idempotence key, covering input checksums, manifest checksum, question-set versions, model name and tool versions, has changed. A driver runs it from a runbook that contains no instruction to read a source, summarize, or decide, and the run records the driver's command log. [paraphrase] Errors: a stage whose input is missing, whose checksum changed, or which depends on an open decision stops and names the stage or decision; a stage never leaves a partial artifact on disk.
- **R2:** Source discovery and fetch run through one adapter contract whose first adapter is Firecrawl. Discovery lists candidate sources per taxon and field for a person to admit into the manifest; fetch records the final URL, content type, raw checksum and markdown checksum for each admitted source, saves a PDF locally before parse, and yields the age-indexed rows of each admitted table against the row count the manifest states. The pipeline uses no adapter option that asks a model to answer or extract. Before this criterion is met, the three parked checks below have run and their results are recorded, and the raw checksum is taken from bytes the adapter fidelity check showed equal to the original response. [paraphrase] Errors: unauthenticated or failed fetch files unavailable-source with the adapter's error; a parsed table with fewer age-indexed rows than the manifest states files coverage-gap.
- **R3:** Evidence fields are filled by the four routes only. The screen and selection tools from fn-57 fill every measured field with source ID, copied span and ledger reference in the sidecar; the fit fills fitted fields; a described trait is scored over described levels, code proposes candidate values from the value table the manifest names, generation renders and measures each candidate, and the shipped value is one code proposed and a render measured, chosen by the fixed in-range nearest-midpoint rule with ties to table order, with level, sentence, candidates, measurements, ranking and ledger reference in the sidecar and no probabilities; a reference-transferred dial names its nearest tuned template and the scored relation, code computes the candidate from that template's shipped value, and the candidate is rendered and measured under the same rule, with reference, level, candidate, measurements and ledger reference in the sidecar. Engineering parameters are marked as engineering with the author's rationale and never as evidence. The packet's closed records gain no keys. [paraphrase] Errors: a field with no admissible candidate is recorded as unavailable with the reason; a missing age curve files missing-curve; a described trait whose value table the manifest does not name, or whose candidates were not rendered and measured, is recorded as unavailable, never mapped by the model; a described trait with no candidate measuring inside the level's range files a decision of kind level-miss for that trait; a reference transfer whose nearest-reference answer is none, or whose only references are of another growth form when the manifest forbids that, files a decision of kind no-reference for that dial.
- **R4:** Packet verification runs fn-57's citation check over every claim in the filled packet against its source section, runs the semantic obligation questions this spec adds, and checks the structural obligations in code. Contradicted, unsupported and unmet items become decisions of their kinds. Each question set this spec adds, source ranking, described-level scoring and semantic obligations, is versioned data with a labelled set and an output schema. [paraphrase] Errors: an unreachable source lists its claims as unchecked; a structural failure names the value and the rule.
- **R5:** Curve composition and fit run in code from the selected points with the objective fn30 recorded, squared log error at the manifest's three reference ages over the grid fn30 used, shape from 1.0 to 8.0 in steps of 0.1 and rate from 0.001 to 0.399 in steps of 0.001 within each trait's validated range, the first minimum in grid order taken on a tie, the 15 percent tolerance the onboarding doc sets, linear interpolation between table rows, no extrapolation beyond a table's last row, and unit conversion in code. Height and trunk diameter are tested independently at all three ages, and every proxy and composition choice is read from the manifest. A miss beyond tolerance files tolerance-miss; the pipeline never accepts one. [paraphrase] Errors: fewer than two age-indexed points for a dimension files missing-curve and skips that fit; a needed age beyond the last row is recorded as unavailable, never extrapolated.
- **R6:** A model-swap test runs the whole pipeline in two isolated working directories with no shared cache, once driven by the host model and once by a named cheap model from the repo's routing, over pinned source fixtures, one admitted manifest, pinned question sets and one recorded model name, pinned to a version when the service exposes one and otherwise with the two drivers' trials interleaved in one window, for the oak and the spruce, three trials each. The canonical artifacts, packet, sidecar, decision list and report, are byte-identical across drivers and trials, where the sidecar and decisions carry chosen options, levels, copied values and ledger references but no probabilities, and the ledger itself is excluded from the comparison, and each driver's command log contains only runbook commands. The test is excluded from the workspace test commands. [paraphrase] Errors: a difference is reported per artifact and per JSON Pointer; a command outside the runbook fails the trial and names it.
- **R7:** The pipeline is validated on the oak and the spruce against expected artifacts derived from fn30 and checked by a person. Every difference from the expected artifacts carries a disposition a person records, fn30 error, pipeline error or accepted alternative, and an unresolved difference fails the criterion. Each question set carries a labelled set with negative and held-out cases; the held-out accuracy is at least 0.9 for statement kind, citation relation, sufficiency level, dominant gap, described level and each semantic obligation, top-one agreement with the person's admitted source is at least 0.8 for source ranking, and every set reports its confidence spread. [paraphrase] Errors: a held-out accuracy below the bound fails with the missed cases listed.
- **R8:** A data-quality gate runs after the screen and before select, fit and generation. For every required evidence field it records the measured points found, their condition and taxon, the required ages covered, Jev's sufficiency level over the four described levels and its dominant gap, and it files a data-insufficient decision for every field below the manifest's bar, after which no select, fit or render runs for that field. On the pilot's evidence the Garry oak young-age height field scores proxy only with the wrong condition as its gap, and a field with open-grown points at every required age scores sufficient. [paraphrase] Errors: a field with no screened candidate at all scores none and files the decision with an empty points list; the sufficiency question set carries its own labelled set under R7's bound.

## Boundaries
<!-- scope: business -->

- Jev never writes a number. A parameter reaches a preset only as a copied span, a fit over spans, or a described level mapped through a value table a person authored and the manifest names. Jev checks that the evidence says what the packet claims and picks among options code presents. [paraphrase]
- The manifest is authored by a person and never edited by the pipeline. Discovery proposes; it does not admit. [paraphrase]
- The onboarding gates stay. The pipeline stops before generation with an onboarding-gate decision while capability assessment, registry and binding, or audited seeds are unresolved, and it does not claim a measurement window. [paraphrase]
- Scoring a measured value against a described trait, such as a crown that may equal its height, waits for its own labelled set and is not in this spec. [inferred]
- Every still stays with the owner's eye. The pipeline runs to the point where stills exist and files a visual-unassessed decision, which the report may describe before the final stop. [paraphrase]
- No new generator or renderer behaviour. The pipeline consumes the existing measurement example, headless renderer and preset records. [inferred]
- No gap handling. A capability the generator lacks halts the run as a decision; fn-63-gap-loop-options-escalation-and-resume proposes, routes and resumes.
- This spec ships no species. The oak and the spruce appear only as validation fixtures under R6 and R7; every new species is its own spec under the one-species-per-spec rule. [paraphrase]
- fn-57's caller, ledger, screen, selection and citation tools are consumed, not rebuilt. The three question sets named in R4 are this spec's, built on that caller. [paraphrase]
