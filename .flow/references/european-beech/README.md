# European beech references

## Photographic set (fn-188)

Real photographs from Wikimedia Commons, gathered for fn-188 to judge renders at five scales: S1 whole tree leafless, S2 whole tree in leaf, S3 mid-crown limbs leafless, S4 twig spray leafless, S5 leafy spray. Each file is resized to at most 2000 px on the long side; the licence and attribution are the Commons file page's. The catalogue's existing beech references (`catalogue/european-beech/packet/references.json`, OSU Landscape Plants fasy951/fasy896) carry no redistribution licence, so none is copied here. Species identity is the uploader's caption or category; the two Geograph S3 images are captioned only "beech" (British woodland, where *Fagus sylvatica* is the beech).

`S1-entzia-open-winter.jpg`: S1, whole tree, leafless. An open-grown mature beech on a hilltop pasture in the Entzia range, Álava, February.

A short, stout bole that divides at about a fifth of the height into several ascending leaders; a broad, domed, slightly asymmetric crown wider than tall, with fine twig masses at the periphery giving a hazy outline. Whole tree in frame, frontal, against clear sky.

`S1-rostock-copper-winter.jpg`: S1, whole tree, leafless. A copper beech (*Fagus sylvatica* f. *purpurea*, a colour form with the species' architecture), natural monument no. 35, August-Bebel-Straße, Rostock, January.

A single straight bole to about a third of the height, then many ascending limbs forming a tall ovoid crown; dense, fine, evenly spread terminal twigs make a crisp crown outline.

`S2-nettleden-field-leaf.jpg`: S2, whole tree, in leaf. An open-grown beech standing alone in a field near Nettleden, Hertfordshire, July.

A short visible bole under a dense, dark, rounded-to-ovoid summer crown that reaches nearly to the ground; the lower crown skirts outward. Shows the typical solid, opaque leaf-on beech silhouette.

`S3-branching-limbs.jpg`: S3, mid-crown limbs, leafless. Looking up into the lower crown of a beech in winter (Geograph 6011200, December).

Smooth grey main limbs rising from the trunk, each dividing at narrow angles into secondary limbs, with sinuous, often crossing third-order branches and fine twigs at the frame edges. Good for limb taper, fork angles and the smooth-bark sinuosity.

`S4-zigzag-twig-buds.jpg`: S4, twig spray, leafless. A leafless beech twig against blue sky, Chadwick Arboretum, Ohio State University, 1 April.

The zigzag (sympodial-looking) long shoot with long, slender, spindle-shaped buds borne alternately and distichously at each node; short lateral shoots of one or two buds. The clearest view of beech shoot form and bud arrangement in the set.

`S5-shoot-leaves-lammas.jpg`: S5, leafy spray. A beech shoot with a lammas (second, midsummer) flush, Rimberg, Hesse, July.
