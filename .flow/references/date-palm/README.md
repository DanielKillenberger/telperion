The source, author and licence below are fn-80's `rights.json` beside those files, and they agree with `references.json`; the original files' sha256 match the packet's `asset_sha256`. Each copy is turned upright by its EXIF orientation, resized to at most 2000 px on the long side, JPEG quality 88, metadata stripped.

`P-WHOLE-pond-leaning.jpg`: P-WHOLE, whole tree. Ranaghat, West Bengal: a mature open-grown palm at a pond's edge in fruit.

A single trunk leaning about fifteen degrees and curving back near the top, swollen at the foot; retained leaf bases up its length; a rosette of about thirty arching pinnate fronds, the lower ones drooping well below the crown base into a dark skirt; two hanging date clusters on orange stalks.

`P-TRUNK-orlando-lattice.jpg`: P-TRUNK, trunk and crown base. Orlando, Florida, July 2018, a cultivated tree from below.
