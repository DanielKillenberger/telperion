# Pipeline audit: second representations and parallel chains in the release

Read-only audit of `/home/daniel/Projects/telperion/.worktrees/f4` at `d324db4fb` (release branch, after `64c3db4f5`), 2026-10-16. Nothing was edited, built or run against `target/`.

**The rule** (AGENTS.md "Design principles", `docs/principles.md`, `docs/pipeline.md`): one pipeline whose stages are private; every consumer takes the previous stage's output; no stage is copied outside it; test code compiles only into test binaries; "Question, delete, then optimise". The one sanctioned exception is the GPU executor (`telperion_render::generation`): one algorithm, two executors, through `pipeline::executor`. The owner's correction that prompted this audit (fn-257 spec, line 71 on): the field built its own format from the drawn records instead of reading them.

**Method.** Five grep-based inventories (space engine, core pipeline, renderer, Wasm/TS/harness, spec contracts) on a fast model; one judging pass on a strong model against the sanctioned list, which spot-checked every top-ten line; one adversarial verification of the fourteen load-bearing claims by a different model family (codex, `gpt-6-astra`, read-only), which confirmed 6 and refined 8; and the host's own re-reads of the lines the judge added. Every line number below was opened by at least two of those passes unless marked "inventory only". Costs are lines from `wc -l` unless a measurement is cited; no build or timing was run, so bytes and ms are "unknown" where no evidence file holds them.

**Already owned.** The resident field primitives (`crates/telperion-render/src/generation/resident/field.rs` line 4: "`field.wgsl` makes the field's primitives from the swept nodes", plus `field/{ask,index,make}.rs`, `field.wgsl`, `field_build.wgsl`, `field_query.wgsl`; 1,611 lines, production) are the violation the owner named; fn-257 R8 owns their deletion. They are not ranked here. Its CPU twin is finding 1.
