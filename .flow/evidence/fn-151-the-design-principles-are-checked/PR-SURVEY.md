## Where I was unsure

- **#29 The silver birch (fn-34).** CLAUDE.md says every fn-34 verdict was taken on the growth path that the harness did not show, and the PR ran 25 rounds. That is a P4 breach by history, but I could not confirm it from the diff hunks. The species-name hits outside presets are in test lists.
- **#31 Mature trees are the product; growth is hidden.** This PR puts the growth path behind `?growth=1`. It hides a second path rather than removing it, so it could count as P3 (a flag between ways of building) or as the fix. Left unlabelled.
- **#43 The generator declares what it can express.** `capability.rs` is a declared vocabulary and `gate.rs` files `onboarding-gate` stops. The declaration looks sound, but the gate feeds the stop machinery (PC). This one needs a human call.
- **#66 (closed) A round moves as far as the reviewer says.** `tuning/stride/` adds a Jev-judged step-size class with a stop at `decide.rs:70`. It may be real tuning logic, or it may be a re-qualification layer.
- **#93 (closed) First tuned tree from the sourced profile.** `derive.rs` names no species and is table-driven. But it adds a third route-style JSON table (`profile-to-preset.json`) and writes a derived overlay file. It is P1-adjacent, not a clear breach.
- **#102 (closed) A resolved claim stays resolved.** This PR removes a re-qualification loop, which leans CLEAN. It does so by adding more state to `verify.rs` rather than removing the recheck.
- **#40 The species catalogue as data.** It adds `scripts/catalogue-check.mjs` and `catalogue-pages.mjs`. The inventory lists them as having no Rust caller, but `npm test` and `catalogue:pages` do run them, so they are not dead.
- **#2 and #3 data volume (and #50's 942 files).** These PRs commit about 150k–550k lines of benchmark and run JSON, which later needed archival (#51). That is repo growth for nothing, but it is not clearly what P2 means by "the consumer". I labelled #3 on P3 only.
- **#110, #80 and #78 are closed duplicates** of #114, #112 and #113. #110 also touched `surface/build.rs` and `surface/parallel.rs`, which #114 dropped. That may be a small P1 near-miss, but I did not check it.
