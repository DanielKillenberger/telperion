# fn-224 task 1: insertion and tropism spread (worker, 2026-10-07)

## What is built

- **`Form::insertion_spread`** (per PA, radians, neutral 0, at most pi / 2). Each lateral's and relay's insertion angle is `insertion + spread (2u − 1)`, where u is the axis's own draw, `Key(axis.lineage).child(INSERTION)` with `INSERTION = u64::MAX − 10` (`lineage::insertion`). It applies at the three sites the spec names: `geometry::frame`'s lateral and relay, and `single::bud_frame`. The offset is uniform on [−s, s], so the mean angle is the PA's. At 0 the angle is returned untouched, with no draw.
- **`Form::tropism_spread`** (per PA, a factor, neutral 0, at most 1). Each axis's tropism is its PA's times `1 + spread (2u − 1)`, where u is drawn from `Key(axis.lineage).child(TROPISM)` with `TROPISM = u64::MAX − 11` (`lineage::tropism`). The factor's mean is one. It is fixed once per axis in `Layer::bent`, which now takes it. Both lays that step a walk set it: the final lay (`geometry::lay`) and the rough layout grown with the tree (`grow/draw.rs`). A fine subtree's tallied pencils are never laid and take 1. At 0 the factor is exactly 1, and `tropism × 1 × length` has the same bits as before.
- **GPU.** The PA table grows from 24 to 25 slots. Slots 23 and 24 carry the two spreads (`laid.rs`, `geometry.wgsl`, `lay.wgsl`'s `Pa`). `walk.wgsl` reads `insertion_of` in `lateral_frame` and in the relay frame, and scales `f.tropism` by the axis's draw in `lay_all`. Both use `insertion_draw` and `tropism_draw` (`contract.wgsl`), which mirror `fixed::insertion_draw` and `fixed::tropism_draw`.
- **The closed form (R3): exact in expectation.** `lineage::turn(angle, spread)` gives E cos(a + d) = cos a · sin s / s and E cos²(a + d) = (1 + cos 2a · sin 2s / 2s) / 2 for d uniform on [−s, s]. Every place that read `insertion` as a constant now reads these values (`events::angled`). `Stand` and `Placing` carry `[E cos, E cos²]` in place of the angle. The envelope's and the kept table's along and square terms are linear in the cosine, and the kept parallel moment is linear in the cosine's square (`kept.rs`, `place`), so the formula is exact in expectation, provided the draw is independent of the subtree, which it is: the draw moves no node. At spread 0 the values are `cos_fixed(a)` and its square, computed as before. Tropism never entered the closed form, which takes axes straight.

## For the host

- **The insertion bound is pi / 2.** The worker chose it, for "bounded". An angle offset below zero or past pi turns the lateral through its bearer's axis continuously (`turned` with a negative angle is the opposite azimuth), so nothing jumps, and the closed form's moments hold over the whole range. A tighter bound is a value change in `validate.rs`.
- **The tropism draw is per axis, continuations included.** At a change of PA, a continuation draws its own factor, and the trunk draws one too. The rate can therefore step at a PA boundary, as the PA's own tropism already can.
- **The distribution is uniform for both**, as fn-222's is: bounded, mean-preserving, and with closed-form moments. A correlated or log-normal draw would be a design choice.
