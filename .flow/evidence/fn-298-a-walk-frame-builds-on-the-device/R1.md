- The flag's word (`plan_lay.rs` ~173) is the count laid at the deepest generation past `rec::LAYGENS`, not the depth. It records 1 axis there, so the depth is at least 4 against the 3 recorded. The census in use held depth 1, so the layout grew by 3 or more generations in two cycles, past the `STEP` of 2. That is the cause the spec reads from the code: a projection a cycle late with a fixed slack.
