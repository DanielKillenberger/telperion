# fn-298 R2: what the stepped limits buy

**Verdict: borderline on every stepped limit, so the run stops `NEEDS_HUMAN`.** The combined run is clearly past the line. Each limit alone fails R2's Errors line: either the total and at least one preset sit on different sides of 5%, or the total is within 1 point of it.

## Method

- Scratch patch `raw/r2-scratch.patch` (never committed, reverted with `git apply -R`). It applies to `limits()` in `cycle/build_size.rs`. `R2_FULL` names the stepped limits (`rounds`, `passes`, `laygens`, `sweeps`, `unit_rounds`, or `all`) that are set to `Limits::full(b)`'s value after `.within(b)`. `units`, `draws`, `deposits`, `top` and `tail` are left as computed. With `R2_FULL` unset, `limits()` builds as on the base.
- `build_time` (`--profile ci -p telperion-render --lib build_time -- --ignored`). It covers every shipped preset at its default age and seed, and takes the warm column (each preset's second build in the process). Each run is one process, with `telperion-heavy 8 --threads 2`, on 2026-10-20.
- Every arm built the same instance count per preset as the stepped runs. The limits changed how many loops were dispatched, never what was built.
- Noise band: the spread (max − min) of the three stepped runs in the same batch. "Within noise" means the arm's mean delta is no larger than that spread (R2).

| Limit | Total delta | Rule alone | Errors line | Choice |
|---|--:|---|---|---|
| `rounds` | +2.8%, inside the 147 ms band | delete | beech (+9.7%), birch (+7.0%) and palm (+14.5%) sit past 5% while the total is under it | **borderline, owner** |
| `passes` | +2.4%, inside the band | delete | beech (+5.4%), birch (+7.1%) and palm (+11.5%) past 5% | **borderline, owner** |
| `laygens` | +15.0%, past the band and the line | derive from the job | ordinary (−0.2%) and oak (+4.0%) under 5% while the total is past it | **borderline, owner** |
| `sweeps` | +3.6%, inside the band | delete | beech, birch, palm and telperion (+5.1%) past 5% | **borderline, owner** |
| `unit_rounds` | +4.6%, past the band (one outlier run) | delete | within 1 point of the 5% line; palm and telperion past it | **borderline, owner** |

The readings below are inferred from the numbers, not measured further:

- `laygens` is the limit the reproduced row passed (R1). It is also the only limit whose bound costs the three largest trees (spruce, Telperion, Laurelin) 18 to 20%. Of the five, it is the one where R2's rule points most clearly to a job-derived limit over a delete.
- The other four cost the large trees 0 to 5% and the small trees (beech, birch, palm, about 75 to 215 ms) 5 to 15%, which is 6 to 19 ms. On the 5% line the per-preset reading and the total reading disagree for every one of them, and the spec leaves that call to the owner.
