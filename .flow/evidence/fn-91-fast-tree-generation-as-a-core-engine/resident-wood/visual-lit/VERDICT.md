# Lit exterior wood comparison

The owner rejected the previous shaded, interior views as unjudgeable. Those captures are retained as failed presentation evidence, not acceptance.

This replacement uses four images, one CPU/GPU pair per species at seed 1, the exterior hero camera, bare-wood view, camera-facing sun and neutral brighter sky fill. No generated geometry or materials were edited. Foliage is hidden to inspect wood without needles obscuring it. Camera, scene, backend, Wasm hash and counts are recorded in wood-detail.json. All four images were inspected for framing and illumination before opening the gallery.

The gallery has species buttons, a comparison slider and CPU/GPU-only buttons at full image width. The host opened http://127.0.0.1:8768/wood-lit.html. These images support exterior wood comparison; they do not establish close-up bark, complete foliage appearance, motion or phone qualification. Owner verdict remains pending.
