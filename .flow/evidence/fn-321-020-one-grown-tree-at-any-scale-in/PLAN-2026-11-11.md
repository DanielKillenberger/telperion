# The host's plan to a 0.2.0 release candidate, 11 Nov 2026

Status: **proposed by the host, not approved by the owner.** The owner asked on 11 Nov 2026: "I want you to actually figure out how to get to a satisfying result for a 0.2.0 release. That reaches my stated goals while being engineered cleanly. I want you to run there autonomously ... I only want you to return to me if you're genuinely stuck. I first want you to come up with a plan on how you'll get there which i'll approve and you give me the prompt."

## What went wrong so far, and what this plan does differently

Eight of the host's directions were refuted in two days and no product code landed. The causes:

1. **The model is a web of compensations.** The oak's values were fitted around artefacts: dead wood charged upkeep (the only bound on size), a pipe size fitted so the living crown alone makes the whole trunk, limbs made by timers. Fixing one artefact exposes the next. The host fixed them in the order they were noticed, not in the order they depend on each other.
2. **New rules were invented where the model or the literature already had one** (the radius that never falls, when `retained` existed).
3. **Whole approaches were judged at the end,** against the owner's eye, with no standing scoreboard.
4. **The host lost the owner's rulings** (the device grower's deletion).

So: work in dependency order; before any rule, survey what the code and the sources already hold; keep one fixed scoreboard and land a step only when no tree on it is worse; keep a ledger of the owner's rulings and check each child against it.

## The bar for the release candidate (the host's proposal, for the owner to change)

- **Look.** On a fixed panel (every preset at its preset age; the oak at 40, 80, 120, 200, 300; twelve seeds for measures, three for sheets; whole, mid and close views): no tree worse than today's 0.2 build by the host's eye, with Jev on narrow questions; the oak at 120, 200 and 300 reads as an old oak beside reference photographs on every seed; no tree is refused.
- **Real trees.** Trunk diameter against age and height, height against age, crown width against height and leaf area index inside the published ranges already collected (fn-339), where a source exists.
- **Speed.** Floor: every tree at least 3 times cheaper to grow than today's build in time and memory, and every 0.1.4 species at or under 0.1.4's time in a browser on the CPU. Target, reported and not a stop: the oak at 40 in 200 ms and to 300 in 30 s on one thread.
- **Clean.** One grower, on the CPU; the device grower and today's twig-by-twig foliage path deleted; no value without a source or a listed estimate; the full gate green; a codex review of the whole diff.
- **Delivered as** the integration branch pushed, a draft pull request, and one page for the owner. Merging to master, tagging and publishing to npm stay the owner's.

## Phases, in dependency order

Each phase is one or more flow-next specs built by Opus workers; the host designs, judges and logs. Each phase begins with a survey (what the code already has, what the sources say, arithmetic on a census) and ends by regenerating the scoreboard.

- **Phase 0, the scoreboard and the ledger.** The fixed panel drawn and measured on today's build and on 0.1.4 (browser times included). `RULINGS.md`: every owner ruling with its date and words. A survey of what the harness and the website need once the device no longer grows.
- **Phase 1, one grower.** Delete the device grower and the tests that hold the CPU to it; the harness and the website draw CPU-grown trees (a worker in the browser). The port made on 11 Nov is dropped. Done first because every later rule would otherwise be written twice.
- **Phase 2, wood that lasts.** Land the budget fix (dead wood costs nothing; a living tip pays by its path). Girth as sapwood plus heartwood: the living pipe sized from measured sapwood per leaf area, and laid wood kept, so `retained` stops being a setting. Shed branches buried smoothly (the stepped rings, the open cylinder). Sag reads that wood. Exit: the oak stands at 200 and 300 on twelve seeds with its trunk inside the published range and no young tree worse.
- **Phase 3, a crown that renews.** Sleeping buds that persist on old wood and wake by light, as a real oak's epicormic buds do, with the near-zero-size defect fixed. One bounded attempt (two tries) at the jagged limb line, from the insertion-angle finding of fn-343. Exit: the oak at 200 and 300 carries a living crown to its lower limbs and holds it.
- **Phase 4, counted foliage.** Leafy shoots that cannot leave a light cell carried as counts, giving the structure three sums a year (leaf to shade with, light and charge for the budget, section for girth); a twig built from its key when a view resolves it. Proof first on the oak, spruce and beech. Exit: the speed floor, with the scoreboard no worse.
- **Phase 5, drawn fast and the website.** Levels of detail from the structure's own order; the voxel grove read from the tree's cells; growth off the main thread; the 250 KB budget; page load at or under 0.1.4's.
- **Phase 6, sources and the candidate.** Every preset's rows sourced or listed as estimates (the count lower than today's); the native entry with a headless test; dead code and settings removed; docs and changelog; the full gate, the review, the push, the draft pull request and the owner's page.

## How the run behaves

- **It does not check in.** Progress goes to the goal log and one owner's page that is updated in place; the owner reads them when he wants.
- **It returns only when stuck:** a phase's exit is missed after two different attempts and the host has no third that rests on a source or a measurement; or going on would break a ruling in the ledger; or disk falls under 15 GB.
- **A phase that misses the target but meets the floor goes on** and is reported.
- **Every sheet a verdict rests on is viewed by the host,** and a worse tree is called worse before anything else is said.
- **Unknowns are stated as unknown;** times in the log come from the clock.

## What the host does not know yet

- What the harness and the website need to draw CPU-grown trees with the device grower gone.
- Whether limbs sized from sapwood hold up under sag at 40 and 80.
- Whether counted foliage can stay inside a tenth of the grown tree now that the dead-wood artefact is gone; two earlier reads were 2.5 to 3.9 times cheaper with a biased old oak.
- Whether the speed floor and 0.1.4's browser times can both be met; 0.1.4's times are not in hand.
- How long this takes. It is several days of builder time at least.
