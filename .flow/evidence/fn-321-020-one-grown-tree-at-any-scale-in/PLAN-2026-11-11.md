# 0.2.0 consolidated: what it must be, where it stands, and the plan

Status: **proposed by the host on 11 Nov 2026, not approved by the owner.** It replaces the host's first draft of the same day.

The owner asked (11 Nov): "I want you to actually figure out how to get to a satisfying result for a 0.2.0 release. That reaches my stated goals while being engineered cleanly. I want you to run there autonomously ... I only want you to return to me if you're genuinely stuck. I first want you to come up with a plan ... which i'll approve and you give me the prompt." And: "you need to consolidate all goal specs and reqs we've specified all over the place and come up with a clean plan on how to resolve this."

Sources read for this: fn-237 (the release map), fn-321 and fn-347 (the two goals), every open spec from fn-190 to fn-350, STRATEGY.md, AGENTS.md, RETHINK-2026-11-10.md. Three surveyors read them; their reports are the basis of sections 1 to 3. What they did not read is listed at the end.

Two facts that shape the plan:

- **Speed and fidelity point the same way.** The spruce is 56 times slower than 0.1.4 in the browser and grows 27 times too many shoots. Putting the species on sourced traits removes work as well as error.
- **The per-species coarse rows are what makes the website fast today** (coarsest oak 164 ms in Wasm). They are ruled out (P6), so they go only when counted foliage replaces them.

## 4. What the owner decides when approving

Each has the host's recommendation. Approving the plan as written accepts the recommendations.

1. **The speed bar in the browser.** Recommended: target, each 0.1.4 species at or under 0.1.4's time; the run goes on and reports if a species lands between one and two times 0.1.4's time with a better tree (the owner, 9 Oct: "if we get way better fidelity for slightly less performance that's a trade we can live with"); beyond two times is stuck.
2. **The native speed numbers** (the host's, never confirmed): the oak at 40 in 200 ms, to 300 in 30 s, a thousand oaks at 40 in 20 s. Recommended: keep them as targets that are reported, with a floor of three times cheaper than today in time and memory.
3. **Counted foliage against the 13 Oct rule** ("No speed-up may replace growth with a shortcut that changes the grown tree beyond its measured tolerance"). Recommended: AGENTS.md says that leafy shoots confined to a light cell are counted by the same law and built from their key when seen, and that this is the growth, not a shortcut. STRATEGY.md's sentence on the persistent structure is reworded to match.
4. **The four specs never ruled release or later.** Recommended: fn-279, fn-280 and fn-267 after the release unless the scoreboard shows the fault on a shipped preset; fn-307's repro is run once and decides it.
5. **The film (L5).** Recommended: re-render it from its script once the trees are final; the host screens it and it goes to the owner with the candidate.
6. **Pull request #159.** Recommended: the host opens a new draft from the integration branch and leaves #159 for the owner to close.
7. **Pushing.** Recommended: the host may push the integration branch and open the draft pull request without asking; nothing else is pushed.
8. **Limits.** Recommended: no limit on children; stop when free disk falls under 15 GB.
9. **The Two Trees.** Uncharged by the new budget they match today's look but move on the film's map. Recommended: they take a potential height like any tree, chosen from their base species, and are re-screened.

## 5. The plan, in dependency order

Each phase is one or more children built by Opus workers. Each begins with a survey (what the code already has, what the sources say, arithmetic on a census) and ends by redrawing the scoreboard. A phase lands only if no tree on the scoreboard is worse than at the last landing.

- **Phase 0, one source of truth.** The new goal spec; the closures of section 3; `RULINGS.md` with every owner ruling by date; the format fix. The scoreboard: a fixed panel (every preset at its preset age; the oak at 40, 80, 120, 200, 300; twelve seeds for measures, three for sheets; whole, mid and close views), drawn and measured on today's build, with 0.1.4's browser times and stills beside it.
- **Phase 1, one grower (P5).** Delete the GPU grower and its tests. The harness and the website draw CPU-grown trees, in a worker in the browser. The 11 Nov port is dropped. First, because every later rule would otherwise be written twice.
- **Phase 2, wood that lasts (P2, P8, L6).** Land the budget fix. Girth as living sapwood plus kept heartwood: the pipe sized from measured sapwood per leaf area, laid wood never lost, so the `retained` setting is deleted. Shed branches buried smoothly (the stepped rings, the open cylinder). Sag reads that wood. Exit: the oak stands at 200 and 300 on twelve seeds, its trunk inside White's range, no young tree worse.
- **Phase 3, a crown that renews (P2).** Sleeping buds that persist on old wood and wake by light, as an oak's epicormic buds do. Two tries, no more, at the jagged limb line, from the insertion-angle finding (fn-343). Exit: the oak at 200 and 300 holds a living crown down to its lower limbs.
- **Phase 4, species on sources (P6, P15).** Each real species' shoot and leaf tables re-derived from measurements: the spruce's shoots a year, the beech's leaf area. Every row sourced or listed as an estimate. Exit: P15's measures in range where a source exists, and the cost that falls out is recorded.
- **Phase 5, counted foliage (P1, P3, P6).** Leafy shoots that cannot leave a light cell carried as counts; a twig built from its key when a view resolves it. A proof on the oak, spruce and beech before the product is touched. The per-species coarse rows are deleted here. Exit: the speed bars of section 4, the scoreboard no worse.
- **Phase 6, drawn fast, the website and the entry (P1, P10, P11, P13).** Levels of detail from the structure's own order; the voxel grove read from the tree's cells; growth off the main thread with or without WebGPU; the 250 KB budget; the native entry with a headless test; the forest rig and the frame measures.
- **Phase 7, the candidate (L1 to L7).** Dead code and settings removed; docs and changelog; the film; the full gate; the release checks; a codex review of the whole diff; the push, the draft pull request and one page for the owner with every preset beside 0.1.4 and today's.

## 6. How the run behaves

- **It does not check in.** Progress goes to the goal log and to one owner's page updated in place.
- **It returns only when stuck:** a phase's exit is missed after two different attempts and the host has no third resting on a source or a measurement; or going on would break a ruling in `RULINGS.md`; or disk runs low.
- **Before any rule is built** the host checks `RULINGS.md` and what the code already has, and derives the expected result on paper.
- **Times in the log come from the clock; unknowns are written as unknown.**

## 7. What is not known

- What the harness and the website need to draw CPU-grown trees once the GPU grower is gone.
- Whether limbs sized from sapwood hold up under sag at 40 and 80.
- Whether counted foliage stays close to the grown tree now that the dead-wood artefact is gone, and whether it reaches 0.1.4's browser times: the gap today is 5 to 56 times.
- How long this takes. It is at least several days of builder time.
- Not read by the surveyors: the evidence files the older specs cite, five specs' later sections (fn-301, 304, 308, 309, 310), clippy's state, whether oak seed 7 was ever fixed, and whether P13 needs the bark texture (fn-247).
