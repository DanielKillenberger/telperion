## The gate and npm test (measured, 2026-10-21 00:11 to 00:52)

`cargo test --profile ci --workspace --no-fail-fast` under `telperion-heavy 8 --threads 2` took 2,167 s: 1,417 passed, 6 failed, 111 ignored (`raw/gate.log`). `npm test` passed: 22 files and 177 tests, in 287 s (`raw/npm-test.log`). All six failures are walk tests in `telperion-core`. The causes below are read from each failure's message and the rule's mechanism; none has been traced step by step.
- `catalogue_identity::every_walk_is_the_walk_it_was` pins a digest of every pair's walk at t = 0.13, 0.5 and 0.731. Rule B changes those frames, so the digest moves (14921577383209711129 against the pinned 16363652001628328573). This is the intended change, and the pin needs re-pinning.
- `walk_law::every_film_walk_changes_its_leaf_forms_by_degree` (fn-277's law, host ruling 5a) fails on "cluster leaves per short shoot". At ages 12 to 14 the count moves by 1.0 in one step of 0.001, right at an end. Examples: spruce to birch, age 14, at t = 0.001; birch to oak, ages 12 and 13, at t = 0.001; beech to spruce, age 14, at t = 1. Rule B swaps every row of an age one end never reaches, so the other end's `cluster_leaves` arrives whole at t = 0⁺, while the bud's chance of entering that age fades. The law reads the rows per age, unweighted by reach, and refuses a switch. Oak to palm stays within the law.
- `walk_leaves::spruce_to_birch_changes_its_leaves_by_degree` reports "short shoots of unlike counts: [0.486, 3.0]". Every short-shooting age of a species must share one `cluster_leaves`. Rule B gives a swapped age the birch's 3.0 whole, while the ages both ends reach mix to 0.486.
- `space_walks::a_walk_to_the_palm_is_never_grown_to_its_ends_size` (host decision 1, 2026-10-08) expects the oak-to-palm frame at 0.5 to keep the blended species unscaled. With the oak's later ages kept whole, the predicted size apparently rises, and fn-230's size factor now shrinks the frame's laterals (inferred from the assertion: the walked species differs from the raw blend).
- `space_walks::the_grown_count_binds_where_a_blend_sheds_more_than_its_ends` (host decision, option A): at beech to spruce at 80 and t = 0.75, the grown share is now 0.949, where the test expects 1. The grown count no longer binds at this point.
- `walk_sizes::the_teaser_frame_fits_and_the_named_exceptions_grow_no_further_out`: the named exception (Oak, Birch) at 80 and t = 0.01 now grows 3,953,617 kept phytomers, further outside its band than before.

The last five tests are owner or host decisions on walk properties (the leaf law, the shared cluster count, the size factor, which count binds, the named size exceptions). They are reported to the host, not edited.
