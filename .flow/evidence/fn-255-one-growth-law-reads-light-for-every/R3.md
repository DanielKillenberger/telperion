# fn-255 R3: the foliage-volume candidate against the lattice (worker, 2026-10-11)

**Ruling (council, 2026-10-11, `fn-245/COUNCIL.md`): the candidate is refused, and the lattice is the light model for every species.** The candidate's code is kept only as evidence in `candidate/`, wired into nothing. It is the code these measures ran: the volumes, its tests, the probe, the cycle as it read them, and the two examples. The cycle in the crate reads the lattice alone.

This is the stop at R3 for the owner's decision. The design is in DESIGN.md. Nothing is deleted yet: the lattice, the stepped executor and `reads_light()` all stand, and production (`grow`, `grow_marked`) is F4's.

## Setup

- **Built:** `grow_cycle` (R1 and R2) and the candidate, `light/volumes`.
- **Measured by:**
  - `crates/telperion-core/examples/light_volumes.rs`
  - `crates/telperion-space/examples/amplify.rs`
- **Runs:** `--profile ci` on cores 0 to 3, one process at a time.
- **Raw output:** `raw/` (ignored).
- **Light:** the oak is the shipped preset under its own light (extinction 0.5, sky 0.5).

### The candidates

A candidate is "leaf c / near / far":

- **c** is how far one leaf's kernel reaches, in the PA's leaf size `sqrt(leaf_area)`. For the oak that size is 0.0425 m.
- **near / far** is the refinement interval, in the aggregate's reaches.

| c | What it is |
|---|---|
| 1 | The spec's floor read literally: a leaf reaches its own size. |
| 4 | Four leaf sizes. |
| 11.75 | Reaches 0.5 m, the lattice's own cell. This separates the hierarchy's error from the smoothing scale. |

## R1 and R2: the one cycle

All three checks pass (`grow::cycle::tests`):

- an unlit species through the cycle grows today's tree;
- the oak through the cycle under the lattice agrees with today's build within `agree::PATHS`;
- zero and 1e-30 grow the same tree, for extinction and for the response rows, under both models.

Every shipped preset grows through the cycle at its default age, seed 42, and every one keeps the same tree as F4. That covers kept phytomers, height, crown and crown base (`raw/cost-default-42.txt`). The cost differs:

## Fusion

From the dispatch graph:

- **Per group:** the moments fuse into the layout pass that emits the leaves.
- **Per subtree:** the sums need a reduction of their own each cycle. The light read must finish before the next balance, which sums that light.
- On Astra's strict reading this is a miss: one extra additive O(axes) reduction per cycle. The build costs 3.8 to 4.8 s over the run at 80, against 0.47 s for the whole lattice.

## For the owner

1. **No setting of this candidate is both faithful and cheap.**
   - **Leaf 1** is close to fast on the CPU (light 25 s against the lattice's 0.5 s at 80). It halves the oak at 80 and lifts its crown base from 5.1 to 7.5 m.
   - **Leaf 11.75** holds the oak: kept −7% and the same form at 80, with stills close to F4's. Its light takes 476 s against 0.5 s, about 1,000× the lattice. Its worst bud visits about 11% of all nodes.
2. **The lattice is cheap on the CPU at these sizes:** 0.05 s for the oak at 40, 0.47 s at 80. The candidate's case rests on the GPU executor (fn-256), where the lattice's deposit and sweep are the extra passes. On the CPU the candidate is not a speed gain at any measured setting.
3. **The cost of the one cycle on the CPU,** with the lattice, is 4 to 18 times F4's build for the species that read no light today. Telperion grows 18.2M of a 20M budget.
4. **Open, for the host:**
   - whether to approve the candidate as built;
   - whether to try a spatial hierarchy (by position, not by botany) over the same kernels, before any approval;
   - whether the kernel floor should be the lattice's scale rather than the leaf's.

   Each is a design judgement this worker does not make.
