# fn-241 R2: the field test's time

**Where the time goes.** Measured on the pre-merge base, seven presets, with timing logged by temporary instrumentation (`raw/r2-timing.log`). The test took 54.4 s in all. In ms, per preset:

| Preset | slim `growField` | full `engine.build` | compare |
|---|---|---|---|
| ordinary | 1,890 | 1,907 | 37 |
| oak | 2,540 | 2,512 | 119 |
| spruce | 8,550 | 8,500 | 1,309 |
| beech | 296 | 281 | 61 |
| date palm | 8 | 9 | 10 |
| Telperion | 7,052 | 6,769 | 2,043 |
| Laurelin | 4,653 | 4,416 | 1,384 |

- **Growth is almost all of it.** Each preset grows twice, once through each entry. The two chains are what the test compares, so neither growth can be shared.
- **The grid queries** are under 4 s together.
- **Growth is sequential Wasm.** Running presets concurrently in one test would not run them at the same time.

**The change.** One test a preset (`it.each` over `PRESETS`), with the slim module and the full engine made once and shared. Each test is still both growths, the bounds and the four byte-for-byte arrays. Every preset is still covered, and the `180_000` override is gone, so each test runs under the config's 60 s.
