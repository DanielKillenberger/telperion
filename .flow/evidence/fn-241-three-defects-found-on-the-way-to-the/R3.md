# fn-241 R3: the cold build's margin, measured after the F4 merge

All runs: `browser_route` for the beech at 20, cold (`__GL_SHADER_DISK_CACHE=0`), `TELPERION_UNSOWN=1`, under the GPU lock, one process a run, as the cold-build test runs it. Logs are in `raw/r3-*.log`. Bound: 5 s besides `grow`, so a 1.5 s margin means at most 3.5 s.

**Where the time besides `grow` goes** (merged base):
- **Other pipelines, 3.9 s:** `girths` 1.07, `asks` 1.07, `lays` 0.35, `bends` 0.35, `dress` 0.27, `assign` 0.14, `bloom` 0.10, `place` 0.09. 66 others make 0.85 s together.
- **The build itself,** after every pipeline is made: about 0.85 s (submits 1 to 44 in the trace).

**What the two kept changes do.** Neither changes an output. Chain, realized, mirror and stepped feedback parity are green (`raw/r3-parity.log`, 9 passed).
- `units.rs` writes each fractional place's `logit(t << 9)` after the places' thresholds, by the contract on the CPU, as the node bounds already are.
- `assign.wgsl` `take_ask` runs the ask's lead and its place's lead through one `lead_q` and `presence` call site. `place_q` is gone.
- Together they take `asks` from 1.07 to 0.87 s, so 0.1 to 0.2 s off the first tree.
- The `girths` experiment saved 0.05 s and is reverted.

**Why the margin is not restored.** Inlined call sites are not where the time sits any more. Restoring 1.5 s means taking 1.3 s out of the 3.9 s that 74 pipelines take to make, or out of the build. Per-shader slimming yields tens to a couple of hundred ms each.

The remaining routes are design choices, so they go to the host:
1. **Make the pipelines besides `grow` concurrently with it.** On wasm, `createComputePipelineAsync`; natively, threads. The first tree's time besides `grow` would then be the build alone, about 0.9 s. This changes what the native cold test models of the browser.
2. **Raise the first-tree bound.** The bound already exempts `grow`.
3. **Keep slimming shader by shader** (`girths`, `lays`, `bends`, `dress`), each a measured restructure with an uncertain gain.

## Host decision (2026-10-09): delete the aggregate bound

The host set the first-tree bound of 5 s besides `grow` in fn-235's second decision, and nothing outside the test reads it. What protects the browser is the per-pipeline bound: Chrome's GPU watchdog acts on one pipeline's creation, never on a sum. That bound stays at 2.5 s for every pipeline, with `grow` under its named 20 s exception, and it already names the shader at fault when one grows.

The 5 s sum adds up seventy-four compiles. On a loaded machine it measures load more than shaders, and it has now failed three times without anything changing. By docs/principles.md, "Question, delete, then optimise", it is deleted, not chased.

- **The test:** it keeps the per-pipeline bounds and the `grow` exception. It reports the first tree's cold total as a measurement, not an assertion. The report goes to the test output and REPRO.md's table.
- **The two changes already made stay:** the place threshold's log-odds now comes from the CPU table, and the ask shares one `lead_q` call site. Both leave every output unchanged and both reduce `asks`.
- **No further shader slimming and no concurrent creation** in this spec.

R3 is amended to match. Then run the gate, npm test, impl-review and `flowctl done`.
