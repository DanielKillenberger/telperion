REJECT

1. **80% per seed permits failure of the defining features.** Beech and oak can miss both division and major-axis count yet pass 8/10; spruce can miss branch elevation and pass 8/9. **Change:** require the architecture and visual checks below at both seeds. Retain 80% only for supplementary, independently justified bands; count compound rows once, requiring every clause. Missing measurements cannot pass.

2. **Ball, comb and architectural identity are insufficiently tested.** Beech and oak bands overlap in every scored measure. Entzia itself has a broad rounded crown, so roundness alone is not the defect. **Change:** make matched-scale bare/whole review mandatory: differentiated limbs reaching the outer crown, substantial secondary subdivisions, and absence of the repetitive paired uprights seen in probe 6. Require spruce’s persistent leader, tiers and predominantly horizontal branch systems. Require a probe trace demonstrating Troll’s basal reorientation and renewal, versus Rauh’s persistent orthotropic axes; mature silhouettes alone cannot establish those developmental models. [Model definitions](https://algorithmicbotany.org/papers/catm.tree2000.pdf).

3. **Bole and division bands mix projected landmarks with geometric measurements.** The broad bole ranges are plausible specimen targets. However, Rostock’s supposed correction from 0.50–0.54 projected division to 0.45 “true” is unsupported. `leaders2` counts crossing segments, including drooping limbs and repeated crossings, rather than persistent leaders. **Change:** remove the perspective correction; use **0.24–0.56 as a provisional projected beech division envelope**, covering the recorded readings with approximately 0.02 margin. Report `leaders2` separately until it counts distinct persistent paths. Keep oak’s division gap as a hypothesis, not an additional independent vote.

4. **The 0.05-root structural boundary is usable as a diagnostic convention, not a botanical boundary.** It changes with basal swelling and taper, and replaces the old scorer’s `NodeKind::Structural`; the old results therefore cannot justify inherited thresholds. **Change:** retain 0.05 explicitly as a measurement convention, recompute both baselines, and report sensitivity at 0.04 and 0.06. Apply it consistently to all affected wood measures. Keep these cutoffs out of generation.

5. **Laterals ≥1.3/m and generations p50 ≤3 lack valid targets.** The former comes from the shipped beech and is borrowed for oak and spruce. Axis categories are not branching generations. Moreover, historical `classes.rs` takes generation percentiles over **all non-structural nodes**, contrary to the stated terminal-path interpretation. **Change:** make both diagnostic-only; define generations over terminal paths, one observation per terminal, and recompute baselines. Do not preserve ≤3 after changing the population.

6. **`root04` is preferable to the union, but 4–7 photographed paths do not validate 4–7 lateral axes.** The code excludes the trunk continuation and can count nested laterals. The photographs include apparent leaders and hide overlapping paths. **Change:** retain `root04`, `local04` and their union as separate diagnostics; remove the count’s independent acceptance vote until annotated paths establish the correspondence. Explicitly choose the population for secondaries too: historical `score.rs` uses the **union**, even when only `root04` is scored.

7. **Secondary and junction bands compare different populations.** Visible substantial limbs cannot establish the median junction ratio of every first-order axis ≥1 m. Projected secondary reach cannot establish a 3D arc-length ratio. The two inspected S3 images support substantial, irregular subdivisions, but do not resolve complete parent lengths or counts. **Change:** retain the numerical ranges as provisional diagnostics; score matched visible limbs at matched projection. Report per-major secondary counts alongside the median so a comb on one dominant leader cannot disappear in aggregation.

8. **Fine-wood density bands are uncalibrated and partly misnamed.** The scorer measures terminal-run length density, not all fine wood. A photograph cannot recover its volume-normalised shell/interior ratio; spruce needles further obscure wood. **Change:** rename these outputs accurately and remove all six density bands from the acceptance denominator. Judge readable interior limbs and peripheral spray on the sheets. The broad upper/lower “sanity” bands currently add easy votes without establishing architecture.

9. **Spruce elevation is useful, but its sampling evidence is insufficient.** Three selected edge branches do not support a whole-tree share above 45° ≤0.15. “Lowest any lateral” can also pass through one tiny basal shoot. **Change:** retain −5° to +30° as a provisional median target, report the 45° share unscored, and measure the lowest lateral only among axes ≥0.05H. Preserve the photographed spruce’s legitimate pendant comb branchlets; the rejected comb defect concerns repetitive broadleaf leaders.
