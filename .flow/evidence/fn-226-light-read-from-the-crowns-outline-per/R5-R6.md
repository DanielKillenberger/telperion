## The carried walk: no cheap correction found; stopped for the host

`walks::light_changes_the_tree_by_degree` with `WALK=apical_control`, after the deletion: `states[0]` is 31.08 and `states[1]` is 32.05 against `MULTIPLE` = 30. These are R2's values, unchanged (`raw/r6/walk-after-deletion.log`). The bound was not moved.

**What laid quality needs.** The walk's species sets no sag (`Form::default`), so for it a laid position is `place()` without levers. The rough layout's places differ from the laid ones in two ways:
- each unit is bent at its axis's age when it grew, over the reach so far;
- older units are never re-walked.

A laid place of any point therefore needs its whole ancestor chain walked again at the current age. That is `place()` over the tree, which is the bulk of the re-lay. This is inferred from `grow/draw.rs` and the deleted `grow/relay.rs`; it was not traced bud by bud.

**The cheapest correction measured** was the re-lay without its sag passes, every 10 cycles. It was measured on a scratch worktree at `caebae5e`, where the re-lay still exists, under an env switch. The worktree has since been removed.
