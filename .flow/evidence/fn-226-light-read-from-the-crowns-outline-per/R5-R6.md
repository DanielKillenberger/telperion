## The carried walk: no cheap correction found; option 3 taken

`walks::light_changes_the_tree_by_degree` with `WALK=apical_control`, after the deletion: `states[0]` is 31.08 and `states[1]` is 32.05 against `MULTIPLE` = 30. These are R2's values, unchanged (`raw/r6/walk-after-deletion.log`). The bound was not moved.

**What laid quality needs.** The walk's species sets no sag (`Form::default`), so for it a laid position is `place()` without levers. The rough layout's places differ from the laid ones in two ways:
- each unit is bent at its axis's age when it grew, over the reach so far;
- older units are never re-walked.

A laid place of any point therefore needs its whole ancestor chain walked again at the current age. That is `place()` over the tree, which is the bulk of the re-lay. This is inferred from `grow/draw.rs` and the deleted `grow/relay.rs`; it was not traced bud by bud.

**The cheapest correction measured** was the re-lay without its sag passes, every 10 cycles. It was measured on a scratch worktree at `caebae5e`, where the re-lay still exists, under an env switch. The worktree has since been removed.

How the build times were taken:
- **Probe:** the best of 3 `grow` builds of `r2-measure.rs`, at `--profile ci` with `RAYON_NUM_THREADS=8`.
- **Load:** the machine was loaded by other sessions. Starting loads were 4.3 to 9.1, except full s1 at 8.0 (its time reads high). The s7 row is a re-run, after the first s7 builds hit loads of 42 and 67. Raw output is in `raw/r6/oak80.jsonl`.
- **Measures:** s7 and s42 move by a few per cent under the sagless re-lay against no re-lay: crown y 32.96 against 32.08 at s7, and the clear trunk unchanged.

What the measures show:
- **The walk:** for this species the sagless re-lay is exactly the full one, so it restores the base's walk to the digit (`raw/r6/walk-nosag.log`).
- **Its cost:** it keeps 70 to 85 % of the re-lay's cost at s7 and s42. Sag is the small part; assembly, `place()` and the write-back are most of it. It is not cheap, and on the oak, which sags, its places are not laid quality.
- **Within R2's saving:** no correction cheaper than re-walking the tree was found.

**The host's options** (each is a design call, so none was taken):
1. A sagless re-lay, every 10 cycles, at about +15 to +32 % at 80. This puts back most of what R2 deleted.
2. The full re-lay back: R2 undone.
3. Keep today's model and judge the walk's bound. The cause is known (R2.md): a steeper light crossing, a slope that refinement shrinks, not a jump.

**Host decision (2026-10-08): option 3.** Today's model stays, with no re-lay: 15 to 44 % of the build is not worth 3 to 7 % of slope on one dial. The project-wide bound stays at 30. `tests/walk/degree.rs` names an exception, `ROUGH_LAYOUT`, for exactly the two walks `light: states[0].apical_control` and `light: states[1].apical_control`, with their own bound of 33. - Every other walk stays at 30, and the jump check holds the two as it holds every walk.
