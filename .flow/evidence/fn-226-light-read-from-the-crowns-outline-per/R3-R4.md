## What was built

- **`light/outline.rs`** (with `fold.rs`, `chord.rs` and `tests.rs`): section 3's equations in contract A's Q32.
  - The fold: core's folds and least shoulder, z up.
  - 16 cone sections, each chord a quadratic with one `isqrt`.
  - The volume by a 64-point midpoint rule, an even leaf area density, and Beer–Lambert per sky direction, with the sum clamped.
  - `T̄` by S5 (a): 64 heights × 4 equal-area radii × 4 azimuths.
- **`grow/stepped/outline.rs`:** the structure above the cut, grown first through every cycle.
  - Each fine root is an `Aggregate` (S1). It enters its bearer's balance with Q = scale × living × light and C = upkeep × scale × wood (S2). It enters the split with its lit presence.
  - Its light is read at its base (S3).
  - Its expected leaves and its reach-bound ball fold into the outline (S6, S7).
  - Each cycle records the outline, and for each root its shed flag, kept share, memory and incoming vigour.
- **`grow/stepped/ahead.rs`:** every root grown ahead alone from that history.
  - Its split starts from its recorded vigour (S4).
  - Its sizes use `size_against(v, p, ψ, T̄^ψ)`.
  - Its buds read light from the last cycle's outline at their own tips.
- **A light-reading species takes R3+R4 at every extinction, none included** (review round 1). There every bud reads light 1 from an outline with no leaf, so the model is its own limit as extinction falls: the rough layout is grown and nothing is skipped as certain to be shed. Only a species none of whose buds read light builds today's tree.
- **One deviation the host should know of:** the four azimuths of S5 are 0, 90, 180 and 270°, the high ring's. S5 names "the 4 ring azimuths", and the low ring is turned by 45°.

### Why the stem shrinks (inferred from the code and the numbers; not traced bud by bud)

1. **Today a group of one bud is whole to the bit** (`allocation::sizes`). The stem's ages are each one bud, so the compounding of λ = 0.45 along the stem's branching points cancels.
2. **Under R4 the stem's `v/p` meets `T̄^ψ` instead.** At each branching point λ = 0.45 gives the lateral side more than its light's share. Along the stem the main bud's v/p falls well below its light, so (v/p)^1.5 / T̄^1.5 is 0.12 to 0.41.
3. **The tree loses its height, so the crown sprawls.** The laterals of the late orders (PA 12, 14) gain up to 3.6 times in single cycles.

This is the drift the inventory named, measured. Whether `M_g = T̄^ψ` should hold the one-bud orders whole is the host's design question, not the worker's.
