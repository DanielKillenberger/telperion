# fn-226 task 2, R2: the re-lay deleted, measured and viewed (worker, 2026-10-08)

The periodic whole-tree re-lay (`grow/relay.rs`, every tenth cycle) is gone from both executors: the whole-tree grower's `year` and the stepped executor's cycle. Light now reads the rough layout's positions every cycle. What only the re-lay used went with it:
- the stepped executor's assembly and dispersal (`stepped/assemble.rs`);
- the fine roots' re-lay stand (`fine_stands`, `fine_standing`);
- `shed::standing`;
- the re-lay's stage and passes (`Stage::Relaid`, `Pass::Relay*`);
- the partition probe's girth regrouping (`Grouping::girth`, `check_girth`), which only the re-lay's leaf-light girth mean read.

**Visible change: yes, at 80.** By the spec's R2 this is reported before the deletion lands; the task is left `in_progress` for the host.

## How measured

- **Base:** `caebae5e`. **After:** this task's commit. `--profile ci`, `RAYON_NUM_THREADS=8`, this worktree's own `target/`.
- **Oak:** `oak()` under `Light { extinction: 0.5, sky: 0.5 }`, built by `grow` (the stepped executor).
- **Probe:** `r2-measure.rs`, run by `r2-run.sh`. It was copied into `crates/telperion-space/examples/r2.rs` for each build, then removed. The before build also set `girth: true` in its `Grouping`; that field no longer exists.
- **Build time:** the best of three `grow` builds. The run waits for a one-minute load under 8 before each tree. The starting loads were 1.3 to 3.2 before and 3.0 to 4.4 after, except 80 s42 before, at 6.7.
- **Grown:** the stepped executor's counts, from one `grow_stepped` build at cut 1,024. Its tree equals `grow`'s in every row (`stepped_same`).
- **Measures:** R1.md's definitions (crown x / y, the kept tips' extent; crown base live and major).
- **Raw:** `raw/r2/before.jsonl` and `raw/r2/after.jsonl`.

## The walk's cause: light read on the rough layout, not a defect

The same test ran alone (`WALK=apical_control`, `light_changes_the_tree_by_degree`) on the base `caebae5e` with R1's instrumentation (`r1-instrumentation.patch`), in a temporary worktree with its own `target/`. The patch's switches skip the re-lay (`R1_NO_RELAY`) or give every bud light 1 (`R1_FLAT`).

| Run | `states[0]` | `states[1]` | `states[2]` |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| Base | 26.82 (seed 3 at 0.4850) | 29.07 (seed 3 at 0.4850) | 7.65 |
| Base, re-lay skipped | 31.08 (seed 1 at 0.5340, branch 65a877f6…) | 32.05 (seed 2 at 0.5235, branch 8310bb58…) | 1.96 |
| This task (re-lay deleted) | 31.08 (seed 1 at 0.5340, branch 65a877f6…) | 32.05 (seed 2 at 0.5235, branch 8310bb58…) | 1.96 |
| Base, flat light | 0.48 | 0.44 | 0.17 |
| Base, flat light, re-lay skipped | 0.48 | 0.44 | 0.17 |

What it shows:
- **No defect in the deletion.** The base with the re-lay call skipped reproduces this task's walk exactly: every slope, seed, step and branch. The deletion is the base without its re-lay, as the 40 and 80 oaks already showed (seeds 1 and 7 equal R1's `norelay`).
- **The re-lay reaches the tree only through light.** With every bud reading light 1, the walk is the same with and without the re-lay, line for line.
- **The quantity that changes faster is the light the buds read.** With light flat, no step of any `apical_control` walk moves the tree by more than 0.48 per unit, so every step near 30 is light's, at the base's crossings as at this task's. At the steepest crossing (`states[1]`, seed 2, 0.5230 to 0.5235), branch 8310bb58… moves 32.05 per unit with light read and at most 0.44 without.
- **Why it rose:** at age 12 the walk tree's one re-lay, at cycle 10, put the buds of cycles 10 to 12 at their sagged, laid places. Now they read light where the rough layout put them. The steepest crossing moved, and it is 7 to 10 % steeper (31.1 and 32.1 against 26.8 and 29.1). This is the drift case. Per decision 2 it is recorded and stops here; task 3 judges the walk again under per-bud outline light.

Not traced further, within the timebox: which bud's light flips at the crossing, and how far its rough place stands from its laid one.
