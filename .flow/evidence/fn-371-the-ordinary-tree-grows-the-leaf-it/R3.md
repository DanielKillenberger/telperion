- **Drawn leaf.** It changes by −2% to +2% at 40 and −2% to −6% at 80. The spec bounded it at under a quarter.
- **Height over trunk.** It falls from 124 to 154 down to 75 to 93 at 40, and from 75 to 98 down to 47 to 60 at 80. The base (`base-2026-11-11`) reads 61 (1001) and 51 (1007) at 40.
- **Whether the drawn tree reads barer than today's:** that is for the host's verdict. The sheet is `sheets/ordinary.jpg`.
