## What grew since fn-271 (1,128 s)

- **Width, about half the growth.** fn-271's gate ran at the desk's 32 test and rayon threads. fn-274 declared 4 threads at 9 GiB (1,551 s), fn-287 2 threads at 12 GB (2,069 s), and this run 2 threads at 8 GB. The tests that existed at fn-271 cost 1,934 test-seconds now, which at 2 lanes is about 970 s of wall on their own.
- **New tests, 1,059 test-seconds**, by the spec that added them: fn-277 438 (the three `walk_leaves` walks), fn-294 144 (the wasm flower binding 128, the no-length window 16), fn-301 159 (`ground_reach`), fn-257 99 (device field), fn-251 80 (`walk_sizes`), fn-299 43 (`units_bound`, the oak-to-palm frames), fn-297 34 (stalk bounds), fn-298 29 (film walk frames, red), fn-283 20 (`walk_rachis`), fn-276 8.
- The base tests fn-271 timed did not grow in work: the walks binary takes 275 s against 310 s after fn-271, the repeat test 182 s against 190 to 217 s.

## Memory bounds the runner

At 8 GB and rayon 2, twelve tests hold 3.2 to 5.2 GB and `ground_reach` 7.8 GB. Two of them together hold 6.5 to 10 GB, so with the light tests beside them they run one after another whatever the runner. Their own times sum to 1,245 s, and the 32 tests over 1.5 GB sum to 1,914 s. That chain is why nextest with groups (2,270 s) barely beats `cargo test` (2,440 s), and it is the floor at 2 rayon threads in 8 GB until its tests get cheaper or each gets more cores.
