## The test left red for the host

`telperion-space grow::tests::a_tree_grows_the_same_without_what_it_is_certain_to_shed`:
- **What failed:** `beech seed 1: 202565 of 251179 grown`. The test asserts that skipping a tree's certain sheds grows less than 2/3 of the whole ("the shipped trees' twigs are a third of what they grow or more"); the beech now grows 0.81 of it.
- **What still holds:** the equality half held. Without its certain sheds the tree is the same, to the bit.

- **What was not changed:** the test's own fixture already gives a "beech with long-kept bearers" a 0.9 bound. Whether the beech's bound moves to keep the test's promise, or the promise changes, is the host's call, so the test was left as it is.

**The grower's own leaf:**
- **What it is:** the sum of `girth::leaf(state, p, age)` over the settled tree: f32(scale) × f32(size) × f32(leafArea) × clamp(leafYears − (age − cycle), 0, 1).
- **How it is read:** probe-side, no product change. `girth::leaf`, `light::leaf_area` and `Phytomer::size` are crate-private, so the probe reads `size` from the phytomer's derived `Debug` text (`raw/runner/probe/src/engine.rs`, `grower_leaf`).
- **Its ratio:** taken against the crown's projection, the convex hull of the drawn leaves.
- **Units:** the grower's own; the Two Trees' figures carry no drawn scale.
