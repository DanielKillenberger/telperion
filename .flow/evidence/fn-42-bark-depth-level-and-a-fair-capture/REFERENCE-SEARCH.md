### Useful morphology, insufficient resolution

SelecTree identifies the species as Quercus garryana in its [tree page](https://selectree.calpoly.edu/tree-detail/1240) and public [photo catalogue](https://selectree.calpoly.edu/api/photos/1240). Its explicitly labelled [bark original](https://d25fkdmcid2ugy.cloudfront.net/images/1200/40/original/quercus-garryana-bark.jpg) was downloaded unchanged to `.refs/fn42-candidates/calpoly-oak-bark.jpg`. Actual JPEG dimensions: **650 × 567**. Credit: **M. Ritter, C. Stubler, W. Mark and J. Reimer**. SelecTree's application states all UFEI content is **CC BY-NC** (no version stated in the inspected notice), with database attribution required.

Inspection: mostly exposed grey mature bark; distinct irregular plate fragments and deep vertical furrows; little obvious moss. A side-lit curved trunk occupies most of the frame, with background at both edges and a small knot on the right. Useful qualitative morphology support, **rejected as the requested high-resolution source**. The URL says original but the downloaded pixels are only 650 × 567; no upscaling performed. Physical width, lighting calibration and white balance are unknown.

The same source's [trunk original](https://d25fkdmcid2ugy.cloudfront.net/images/1200/40/original/quercus-garryana-trunk.jpg) is saved as `.refs/fn42-candidates/calpoly-oak-trunk.jpg`, **650 × 867**. It was not visually inspected because its resolution alone does not meet the search target. Same credit/license.
