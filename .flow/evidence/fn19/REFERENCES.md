# Reference inventory — fn19-references-v1

The initial cohort admits qualitative real-reference comparisons for both species. It has **no matched quantitative real meshes or competitor assets**. [references.json](references.json) records attribution, content hashes, access limits, scale coverage and the manual admission checks. The six original JPEGs were retrieved, decoded and visually inspected on 2026-09-06; their pixels are not redistributed here.

The [OSU oak page](https://landscapeplants.oregonstate.edu/plants/quercus-garryana) identifies the species and describes its spreading crown and lobed leaves. The [OSU spruce page](https://landscapeplants.oregonstate.edu/plants/picea-abies) supports pyramidal habit, drooping secondary branches and single needles; cultivar descriptions are excluded. [USFS oak silvics](https://research.fs.usda.gov/silvics/oregon-white-oak), William I. Stein (1990), provides habitat context. These sources do not establish distributions of branch order, taper, crown gaps or foliage counts.

Existing fn9 source IDs remain intact. Its profiles retain historical OWIC-OAK, OSU-OAK-ID and NCSU-SPRUCE citations; they are not newly verified measurements in this inventory. Unknown photographic age, dimensions and growth history prohibit calibrated pixel or mesh comparisons. A winter oak image supports woody anatomy only. The spruce montage's two trees must not become one measured crown.

Public images and documentation are discovery evidence. A future acquired mesh requires usage rights, bytes/hash, units, scientific identity, age/context and matched capture conditions before quantitative admission. No purchase, external message or expert feedback occurred.

## Admission and remaining claims

Manual admission opened O-WHOLE/O-BARE/O-LEAF and S-WHOLE/S-BRANCH/S-NEEDLE and checked visible anatomy against their species pages. This establishes usable qualitative comparisons for each species. It does not award a biological pass to generated output. Base/collar detail, bare spruce architecture, measured structural distributions and independent expert assessment remain unavailable or unassessed.

Age and crowded-context gaps are inventoried for fn21 in the JSON. The initial population stays mature open-grown; neither descriptions of young trees nor a commercial seedling change it. A new reference or stronger matching status requires a new benchmark/reference version and leaves this evidence intact.
