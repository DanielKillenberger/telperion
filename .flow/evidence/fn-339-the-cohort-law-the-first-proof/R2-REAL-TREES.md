## Bearing on R1

The spruce's 1,707 new shoots a tree a year at about 36 years is the one shoot count checked. On R1's arithmetic, one spruce branch medial node carries about 18 new shoots a year once its branchlets are a decade old, and a branch unit about 33. A tree's worth of branch units would pass the measured count many times over. That is consistent with the goal's "50 to 70 times", but no tree is grown here, so no ratio is claimed.
