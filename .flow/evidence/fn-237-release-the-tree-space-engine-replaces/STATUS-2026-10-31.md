## Why the release checks are green and the site is not better

- The site left the bar on 15 Oct ("the site is the site's repository"), and matching 0.1.4's per-tree speed left it on 26 Oct, so no release check reads the site.
- The site's own spec, fn-46, asks for the grove to "look the same after the move" (its paraphrase) and to "keep the grove's look". A visible gain there was never a requirement.
- The grove draws at 24, 30 and 36 cells (`src/lib/grove/sets.ts`). Inference, not measured: the engine's fidelity gain does not survive at that resolution. Daniel's look is the evidence so far.
- The costs on the site are measured or recorded: spruce 203-212 ms to 503-539 ms and oak 233-260 ms to 355-642 ms (AUDIT-2026-10-26.md), field Wasm 107,663 to 834,077 Brotli bytes, WebGPU only where 0.1.4 ran on the CPU, and one worker after four ran out of memory.

## Not measured

No paired whole-grove time against 0.1.4 and no recorded grove verdict was found in fn-46. The audit's table is a small diagnostic sample.

## Recommendation

Do not tag 0.2.0. Keep 0.1.4 on `latest` and on the site. Finish and merge fn-319, since it is built and fixes real defects. Put the site back on the bar and make fn-285 and fn-242 the work before a tag, after a side-by-side of the grove on both versions confirms what Daniel saw.
