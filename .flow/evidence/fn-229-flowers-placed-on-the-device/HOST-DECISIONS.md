# Host decisions on R1 (host, 2026-10-08)

The worker's task 1 escalation listed five open choices (FRICTION.md). The host settled them by one rule: florets follow exactly the pattern fn-212 built for leaves. "No added pass" means no added readback or round trip. Extra kernels in the same submission are allowed.

- **D1, the key and the share.** The dress pass writes the phytomer's 64-bit key (two words) into the drawn record, and the sweep carries it in its node because placement reads it. The flowering share joins the device's per-PA table. No binding limit is raised, and nothing is re-keyed: the CPU's florets do not move.
- **D2, the pass.** A second entry point in `place.wgsl`, dispatched in the same submission as the device's leaves.
- **D3, count and order.** Count, then scan, deterministically, folded into fn-212's existing stations scan, with no atomics and no scan of its own.
- **D4, the box.** The device's florets use the tree's combined leaf box (the folds fn-212 already reduces), padded by the largest floret's reach. There is no bounds pass. The CPU block's florets are repacked against the shared box.
- **D5, delivery.** An organ buffer and a mass grid go through the resident handoff to the renderer: the CPU's florets first, the device's after, as leaves are.

The allowed surface widens to `generation/submit.rs`, the dress, sweep and stations shaders and their Rust sides, and the resident handoff. R1 in the spec is amended to match.
