# fn-308 R1: the trace, the curves and three candidates

Run on 2026-10-22 at `911262a6e` plus the probe `crates/telperion-core/tests/walk_midpoint.rs` (ignored tests). Every cargo run went through `telperion-heavy 8 --threads 2`. The drawn tree is `telperion_space::grow(growth(family))`, which is what `pipeline/space.rs` grows. "Share" is the lateral share of wood length (axes of order 1 or more). "Laterals" counts lateral axes that have wood. Logs and full tables are in `raw/` (`r1-*`, `c1-*`, `c2-*`, `c3-*`). The candidate patches are in `candidates/`. None is committed.

## Verdict

The age-2 lifespan inference holds at t = 0.5, but it is not the main cause. The drawn oak is already 93 % gone by t = 0.25 in wood and 98 % gone in lateral count. That happens long before age 2's lifespan passes the tree's 40 years at t ≈ 0.47.

The main cause is the branching rows at the ages the palm never reaches (3 to 14). `Species::canonical` writes those ages as placeholders: lateral and dormant probabilities 0, and readiness copied from the palm's age 2, which is 0. fn-299's rule swaps only the timing rows, so these placeholders still mix linearly. At every order of the oak, readiness × lateral probability shrinks by (1 − t)². The oak branches to order 9, so the crown shrinks geometrically in t. It is a steep front-loaded fade, not a step. The other five walks show no such fade.

### C1: blend the rate, `lifespan` on the `Kept` scale, 1/(1 + L) mixed by value (one table row)

This is the rate with the same one-year floor as the old Log scale. A literal 1/L would hold a pass-through age, where L is 0 at one end, at 0 until t = 1 and then switch.
- **Effect on oak to palm.** The stem enters the crown ages until t ≈ 0.75, where L2 reaches 40. The drawn tree barely moves, because the branching placeholders still damp every order. At t = 0.5, seed 1 gives 16 laterals and 32 m. Seed 42 stops its stem on the continuation draw and draws a bare stem (share 0.022).
- **Still `raw/stills/c1-t0.5.png`.** It shows the base palm with one leader limb beside the crown.
- **What else it moves.** `catalogue_identity` re-pins. In `space_walks::the_grown_count_binds_where_a_blend_sheds_more_than_its_ends`, the test fails and prints 0.623 and 0.882 (base green). ### C2: weight the lifespan blend by each end's share of the tree's age in the stage

The rule is t′ = t·s_b / ((1 − t)·s_a + t·s_b), with s = min(L, age) / age. This needs the age passed into `telperion_space::blend`.
- **Effect on oak to palm.** It moves the wrong way. The palm spends the whole 40 years in age 2 and the oak only 12, so the palm's lifespan dominates: L2 is 84.7 at t = 0.5 and 38.6 already at t = 0.2. The t = 0.5 frame is identical to the base. The still `raw/stills/c2-t0.5.png` is byte-identical to `base-t0.5.png`. Fewer laterals are drawn earlier, for example 450 against 722 at t = 0.25.
- **A switch at the passing end.** At an age where one end has L = 0, s = 0, so t′ = 1 for any t > 0. `space_walks::the_size_is_neutral_at_either_end_and_continuous_off_it` fails: the beech's size at t = 10⁻⁶ is 49,387 against 180,095.
- **What else it moves.** `units_bound::the_floor_counts_the_ages_a_cycle_chains` goes from 4 to 2, so fn-299's pass-through sliver moves. `catalogue_identity` re-pins. `the_grown_count_binds` fails and prints 1.000 and 0.775. Two `space_walks` tests fail only because they compare against the unaged `telperion_space::blend`.

## C4 (host, 2026-10-22): rule B over every row, and the lifespan clamped at the tree's age

- `reaching` gives an age one end never reaches every row of the other end.

### Why C4 still fades at the palm end (traced, `raw/c4-trace-fade.log`)

**The age-2 continuation draw.** At seed 7, t = 0.7 (continuation 0.3), and at seed 1, t = 0.9 (continuation 0.1), the stem ends at its lifespan (apex_end 29 and 36). No continuation axis follows, and with relay_ended 0 it dies. The frame is then one bare stem of 11 to 14 m. That is shorter than either end's 16 m stem, which is a dip in total wood below both ends.
- **Near its threshold the draw fades the crown.** At seed 7, t = 0.55, the continuing stem's crown is near-absent in scale: 12 phytomers over 0.08 m at age 3, and the limb relays at 0.02 to 0.22 m. That is the presence window doing its job.
- **The order of the drop follows each seed's draw.** It comes at about t = 0.65 for seed 7, 0.7 for seed 42 and 0.85 for seed 1, as each seed's draw passes 1 − t.
- **The rest of the gradual shrink is the size factor** (fn-230), now active. Read from the trace's lateral sums against the oak's 1.1 at age 3, it is 0.75 at t = 0.25, 0.61 at t = 0.5 and 0.67 at t = 0.75. It applies at every order, so it compounds by design towards the ends' geometric mean.
- **The shortening crown window is the third part.** L2 rises to 29.8 at t = 0.75, so the crown grows for about 10 years.

## The clamp's premise and its reader (host, 2026-10-22; measured at 5a94b71c7)

The probes are `walk_midpoint::a_lifespan_past_the_age_is_dormant` and `what_reads_a_dormant_lifespan`. The logs are `raw/premise.log` and `raw/premise-2.log`.

**1. The premise holds for a single species at whole ages.**
- **Date palm at 40, seed 1:** age 2 with a lifespan of 150, 41 or 40 builds the same tree (5904527706537372751). A lifespan of 39 builds a different tree (15574376873824781046).
- **Beech at 40:** setting every lifespan of 40 or more to 40 builds the unchanged tree (372805801234146191). That touched age 4 (40) and age 6 (70).

**2. The reader is the lifespan itself, not a reader of dormant rows.** On beech to spruce at age 0.5, t = 0.5, the clamp at 1 changes the end values that are mixed.
- **The spruce's lived lifespans move.** Its 2 at ages 0 and 1 becomes 1. On the log scale against the beech's 0, the mixed lifespan becomes 0.414 instead of 0.732 at age 0, and 0.414 instead of 2 at age 1. Those ages are lived within the first cycle, so the mixed values are not dormant.
- **The closed form at one cycle shows the seed's apex taking another path.** With the clamp, its counts are age 0: 2.49, age 1: 2.90 and age 2: 1.29; the apex reaches age 2. Without it, they are age 0: 4.39 and age 1: 1.88.
- **The seed axis is the first quantity that differs.** It has 3 phytomers with the clamp and 5 without.
- **The size solve is not a factor.** The bare clamped blend grows the same tree as `families`' species (15404461716013039816).
- **The premise covers a single species, not a walk.** A walk mixes the clamped ends, and the mix can land below the age. That is the rule working as at 40, where the palm's 150 becomes 40 and the mix becomes 22.1, a lived lifespan. Under one cycle it reaches ages 0 and 1, which the seed lives within the first cycle.

**3. Rounding.**
- `blend::mixed` passes `f.age as u32` to the species blend. That truncates, so 0.5 becomes 0, and the clamp floor is `max(0, 1)` = 1.
- `families`' `sized` takes the same truncated age.
- The engine's grower refuses a fractional age (`pipeline/space.rs::asked`).
- `mesh::build` at 0.5 builds a tree. The growth module grows whole years up to the ceiling of the age (`space/growth.rs`, `committed`; its test asserts `committed == age.ceil()`), and the probe's one-cycle request reproduces the difference. That build path is inferred from the growth module; it was not traced through `mesh::build`.
