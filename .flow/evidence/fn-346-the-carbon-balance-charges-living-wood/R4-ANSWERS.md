## The measurement (no behaviour changed; census probe on Q only, kept as `probes/oldtree.patch`)

Oak, Q, seeds 1001 and 1007, from 119 to 299.

1. **Every limb and bough's history:** birth year, the year it ended and by what (shed by the balance, abortion, survival draw, end of row, lost with its bearer), and in the year before it ended: its remembered balance, its light and its charge, its length, its tip's path, its living tips, and the share of its own boughs (for a limb) or branches (for a bough) whose balance was above zero. This says whether a limb is lost whole while part of it still pays.
2. **Each limb's shape** at 119, 159, 199, 239 and 279: length, base radius, horizontal reach, the height of its tip against its base, the lowest point along it, whether any of it lies below the ground, and its tip's yearly extension over the decade. Say what the sag rule reads (length, girth, load) and what share of the droop it gives.
3. **After a limb is lost:** in the 20 years after, how many new framework axes are born within 3 m of path of its base, and how many sleeping buds the trunk line holds.
4. **The tree's reach:** every 20 years the longest path, the height, the spread, and the share of living tips by height band. Where does the leaf sit as the tree ages?
5. **Q on the other presets,** at each one's preset age and the beech at 80: height, spread, trunk, leaves, held phytomers, peak memory and one-thread seconds beside today's, and one sheet of stills, leafless and leafed, today's beside Q. Also Q's one-thread seconds and peak memory for the oak at 40, 80, 120 and 200.
