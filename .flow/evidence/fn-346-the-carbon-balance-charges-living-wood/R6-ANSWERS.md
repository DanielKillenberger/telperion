## What is wrong now, and it is one thing

The tree has no bound on its extent. Height still gains 3 to 4 m every 20 years at 299 and the spread is still growing. The limbs then run 13 to 40 m, and sag lays them down: with sag off they stand. The collapse is the consequence of the extent, not a second fault.

Why there is no bound: under the charge a tip pays while its light is at least L / `L_max`. The tips at the edge of the crown stand in nearly full light (0.95), so they pay until their path is nearly `L_max`, which the host had doubled to 69.6 m. The doubling was the host's answer to a crown that starved at `L_max` = 34.8 m. So one straight line cannot do both: 1 / 34.8 a metre starves the crown's depth (a tip at 20 m of path would need light 0.57 where a clothed crown stands in about 0.3), and 1 / 69.6 a metre lets the edge run to 66 m.

## The charge's form, restated

The straight line was the host's assumption and it is the weaker one. It says the living wood a leaf keeps is its path times a fixed cross-section. A tree does not hold that cross-section fixed: the longer the path, the more sapwood area a unit of leaf keeps, which is how it holds the water supply to a leaf as the path lengthens (the measured rise of the ratio of sapwood area to leaf area with tree height; McDowell et al. 2002 is the source to check). If the supply to a leaf is held, the cross-section rises in proportion to the path, and the living wood a tip keeps rises with the square of its path:

    cost of a tip = size × (L / L_max)²        L_max = the species' potential height, undoubled

- A tip pays where its light is at least (L / `L_max`)². At 10 m of path on the oak that is 0.08, at 20 m 0.33, at 30 m 0.74, and 1 at 34.8 m.
- The factor of two on `L_max` is withdrawn. It was an estimate standing in for this curvature.
- The exponent 2 is what holding the supply gives. It is listed as an estimate with this reason, since trees compensate in part and not in full; it is not moved to a sheet.
- Everything else is as QR: dead wood nothing, the whole path charged, the main line tested, the balance counting what would remain.
