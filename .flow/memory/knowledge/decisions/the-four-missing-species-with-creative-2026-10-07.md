Owner, 2026-10-07: "I would have you build the 4 missing tree species. They don't need to look the same as today's harness implementations. Ideally they look better. It's difficult to define how to make telperion and laurelin look. I give you full creative freedom to make them look as much as Tolkien would have envisioned them!"

The four presets without an engine species (silver birch, ordinary, Telperion, Laurelin) are each onboarded as engine species, one spec each. Today's harness look is not the bar; looking better is. For Telperion and Laurelin the host has creative freedom, aimed at Tolkien's own descriptions (The Silmarillion, "Of the Beginning of Days"), and judges them as the other species are judged (host first, Astra independently on the same sheet). Generator gaps they need (the supernatural bias field, emitted light) are their own specs, per the one-species-per-spec rule.
