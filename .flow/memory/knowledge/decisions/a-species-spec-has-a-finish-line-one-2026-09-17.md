## Decision

A species spec is one species, one authored manifest, one run of the onboarding method, and a checklist of traits the owner ticks per view at three seeds. It closes when the checklist is ticked. It never closes "on an accepting verdict" with no other finish line, and it never bundles species.

## Why

fn-34 was written for three species and closed only on accepting verdicts. Every owner note minted a spec, fifteen capability dependencies grew under it, and twenty-three rounds chased centre brightness, box fill and an ellipse residual that a round-23 trial showed cannot see the lumps the owner sees. The beech's references were qualitative matches, two of its photographs were different trees, and its real gap, Troll's model of plagiotropic sympodial axes against a monopodial broom in a shell, had no name until the path review of 2026-09-16. The ash moved to fn-56, the beech to fn-62 with a per-view trait checklist, and the birch alone finished at round 26.

## The rules this set

- One species per spec (AGENTS.md, owner, 2026-09-16).
- At most two value rounds per verdict before a generator spec or a stop.
- Jev classifies an owner note as a value row, a generator gap or a tone issue and picks the checklist traits a verdict touches; it does not run row-mapping trial loops where a note names a missing row.
- A trait the reference cannot show, such as a base the matched cameras never frame, gets its own view in the judging set.

## Applies when

Writing or planning any species spec, and deciding whether an owner note is a value round or a new spec.
