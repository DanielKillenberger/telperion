A generator round is judged first by its stills beside the reference photos and today's tree, and the owner sees them before the next round starts. A numeric band that passes while the stills look further from the photos than today's tree is not progress.

**Why:** fn-190 ran eight host-steered probe rounds (2026-10-03). The worker reported the visual gate as failed every round, but the host's reports led with what the numbers passed, never viewed the images, and never showed them to the owner. By R1a round 3 every numeric vote passed, and the owner judged the result "clearly not even close... a huge regression" against today's trees. The owner removed the four-image cap the same day.

**How to apply:** every round of generator work ends with the stills sheet (reference, today, the change) in front of the owner; the host looks at it before reporting; a visual fail ends the round as a fail whatever the scorecard says; numbers are diagnostics that explain a look, never the verdict. Prefer changing today's generator in small steps, each shown, over replacing it wholesale.

**Host screens first (owner, 2026-10-03):** the host views every sheet and fails the obvious ones itself; the owner sees only sheets the host judges plausibly read as their species, with a one-line note of what failed and why. Asking the owner to judge a sheet that is plainly not a tree wastes their attention (fn-190 stage 1, "what's the point of showing me this").
