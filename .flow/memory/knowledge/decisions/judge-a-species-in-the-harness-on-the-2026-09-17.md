## Decision

A species is judged where it will be seen: in the harness, on the mature path, with a base close-up and a view from inside the lower crown beside the matched shots. A verdict taken only on posed, sunlit matched stills is not a verdict on the tree.

## Why

fn-34 took twenty-six rounds of matched-shot verdicts on the silver birch. The owner accepted it at round 25 on the judging page, opened the harness, and saw a different tree: a ring at the fork, whorls of flat limbs, a twig thicket. Two causes, both invisible to the page. First, the matched cameras frame the whole tree from thirty metres and never the base or the inside of the crown, so three real defects passed. Second, the harness drew the growth path (a specimen grown to the preset's age, 590,410 nodes at seed 1) while every still and the numeric protocol built the mature tree directly (73,337 nodes). Every verdict had been taken on a path the harness could not show.

## What changed

fn-65 made the direct build the product and hid growth behind `?growth=1`. Round 26 added the S-FORK and S-UNDER views to the birch's judging set and the owner's acceptance was retaken in the harness itself.

## Alternatives considered

Rendering the judging stills through the growth path instead: rejected because the growth path had no calibrated species and no judging rig, and everything already calibrated lived on the direct build.
