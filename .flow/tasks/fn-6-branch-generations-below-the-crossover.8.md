## Description
The owner's R7 pass on task 6's build returned "not yet" with two findings, and both trace to the pass's anatomy rather than to a preset value. First, a twig is emitted as a single 20 mm internode and placement puts one leaf per internode, so leaves equal twigs exactly (19,744 on Telperion, 40,781 on Laurelin), below the tuft's 137,000 and far below the botanical range; the twig anatomy has a diameter and an internode but no length, so the pass cannot grow a shoot. This task gives the twig a length, and separates a branch's geometric resolution from how many laterals it bears.

### Second finding, from the owner's close-up (2026-09-05, added before the second dispatch)
The owner looked closer at the branching on the task 6 build and sent a picture: thin straight rods leaving a metre-thick limb, some with a leaf cluster at the end, "some don't look natural". Measured on the shipped presets, three causes:
- **Laterals leave inside the turn-limit cone, not at the branching angle.** Every lateral handoff's departure is clamped by `limitTurn` to the growth turn limit: 26° on Telperion, 46° on Laurelin (p10/p50/p90 of the measured departure angle collapse onto those numbers), where the anatomy asks 45°. That was fn-5's "a stiff tree gets its stiffness" choice; it is wrong botanically. A bud's departure angle is a property of the bud (Weber & Penn nDownAngle 30-60°), the turn limit governs curvature along the internodes after it. On Telperion a lateral at 26° runs nearly parallel to its 0.8 m limb, so its own laterals sprout from just outside the limb's surface and read as pins stuck in a log.
- **Every lateral is identical.** Radius ratio 0.304 and length ratio 0.4 at every handoff, three straight internodes each, so second-generation laterals off a limb are 7 cm rods a few metres long, all alike. Real trees vary bud by bud.
- **The internode coarseness already named above** makes the rods straight.

Three more changes, in this task's scope:
- **Departure angle is the anatomy.** A lateral's wanted direction is the branching angle off the parent axis at its whorl position, passed through the bias field; `limitTurn` is applied relative to that wanted direction (so the field cannot swing it more than the turn limit), never relative to the parent's direction. The leader keeps the old rule. The continuity suite's direction criterion distinguishes the two: a leader handoff turns no more than the turn limit; a lateral handoff departs within the turn limit of the branching angle. Record the measured departure distribution before and after.
- **Seeded variation.** Two new stated dials with rails, `angleVariation` (degrees, rest measured, 10 is the starting point) and `vigourVariation` (a fraction applied to a lateral's length ratio and, through the law, its radius; rest measured, 0.15 is the starting point), drawn per node from a deterministic hash of the node index and the seed, never from draw order, so the tree stays byte-identical from its seed and R8's per-node property holds (a node's laterals are the same whether it is a tip or a limb). Zero variation reproduces the unvaried recursion byte for byte.
- **The junction.** Check, and record, how the swept surface joins a 7 cm lateral to an 80 cm limb; if the socket flare reads wrong at that ratio, note it for fn-4 rather than fixing it here.

### Approach
- **The twig has a length, as ONE edge.** `TwigAnatomy` gains `length` (metres, rest about 0.25 m, sourced as a current-year broadleaf shoot; the same selected-anatomy caveat as the other terms). The pass emits a twig as a single skeleton edge of that length at the twig's fixed radius, twig-marked, exactly as it emits a twig today except for the length. It does NOT grow one node per internode: the second dispatch did, at thirteen nodes per twig, and half a million twig nodes hit the ceiling before a single branch was counted. The canopy's station walk (`src/canopy/place.ts` ~323-330) already places `ceil(length / internodeLength)` stations along an edge at `stationsPerInternode` each, so a 0.25 m twig edge receives about twelve leaves with no skeleton cost. Expect leaves to multiply by about twelve: on the order of 240,000 and 490,000. Record the counts against fn-5's R5 range (10^5 to 10^7).
- **Laterals per branch, not per internode.** `laterals` becomes the count of side branches a branch bears along its length (rest measured; 2 is the starting point, matching the topology task 1 estimated), placed at evenly spaced internode stations with the phyllotactic rotation, never at the branch's first internode. The pass's node estimate in `grow.ts` follows: N = internodes + laterals × N_child + twig internodes.
- **Internode length follows the wood.** Replace the fixed `internodes` count with an internode-length law in `law.ts`: `internodeLength(radius) = internodeFactor × 2 × radius`, floored at the twig's internode and capped so a branch has at most 32 internodes; `internodeFactor` (a multiple of the branch's diameter, rest measured; 2.5 is the starting point, giving about 2 m on 79 cm wood) is a stated dial. A branch's internode count is `max(1, round(branchLength / internodeLength))`. The bias field and turn limit are consulted per internode as before, so a finer internode is a smoother branch.
- **Rest values by measurement, per preset, against the ceiling and the build.** With single-edge twigs, sweep `internodeFactor` (1.5, 2.5, 4, 6) and `laterals` (2, 3) on both presets: nodes before and after shedding, twigs, leaves, capped flags, growth time and the harness build time. Each preset states its own `internodeFactor` rest: Telperion is the tree the owner found too coarse and takes the finest factor that fits; Laurelin's dome hides coarseness ("seems okish") and may rest coarser. A preset fits when it is uncapped under the 250,000 ceiling, meets task 5's fill floors, and builds in the harness in under about 3 s. Conductor's decision, so this does not cost another cycle: if Laurelin cannot meet the fill floors under 250,000 at any factor that still resolves its branches, `NODE_CEILING` may rise to at most 400,000 provided the harness build stays under 3 s at the chosen rest; record the number, the build time and the reason in §Measured and in the constant's comment. Beyond that, report rather than choose.
- **Re-measure R4 and the continuity suite** on the chosen rest; thresholds from task 5 stay unless the metric moves, in which case record the new values and why.
- Harness: `angleVariation` and `vigourVariation` get dials with rails and measured comments; the `internodes` dial becomes `internodeFactor`; `laterals` keeps its name with the new meaning and comment; the read-out gains leaves placed.
- Fixture migrations under the standing authorization: any test asserting the old `internodes` semantics or one-leaf-per-twig is rewritten to the new intent; never a widened budget.
