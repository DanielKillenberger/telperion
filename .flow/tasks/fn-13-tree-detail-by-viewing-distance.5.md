## Implementation scope note

The real preparation probe found that full branch structures alone exceed 6 GiB across the pinned forest, before foliage. Task5 therefore includes a compact distant compiler/worker representation with original full-source SHA256 identity and bounded lazy restoration of the unchanged hero/near representation. Shared runtime selection culls by conservative bounds and bounds active detailed preparation; failed preparation keeps valid distant coverage and reports its error.

Hardware probes exposed GPU clock ramp after screenshot pauses. The conductor authorized one fixed recorded 240-frame conditioning batch before the unchanged 8 warmup/120 sample timing protocol. All earlier diagnostics remain preserved; conditioning does not qualify desktop contention or alter the 2 ms/4 ms budgets.

Forest resource sizing was pinned before the actual 1,024-tree construction: 4 GiB retained CPU arrays and a 1 GiB shared GPU geometry pool on RTX 3080 10 GiB (1,450 MiB occupied at the sizing snapshot). The hero path retains its existing 128 MiB pool. The measured small-tree arrays predicted that the hero-sized pool would otherwise force unnecessarily coarse forest cuts. The original 2 ms/4 ms timing and quality targets are unchanged.

The actual population reached 199 prepared specimens before specimen200 exposed catastrophic cancellation in a 24.923µm span encoded at metre-scale Float32 coordinates. Task5 includes a precision-bounded canonical frame validation fix, retaining the original source arrays and rejecting deviations beyond their endpoint rounding uncertainty. The failed run is preserved; it is not population acceptance.

The completed 1,024-tree motion probe exposed the hard two-full-specimen detail limit. Task5 now includes region-filtered canonical preparation, packed fine aggregates with complementary coarse exterior coverage, private immutable selector sharing, and a unique-backing-array guard across the whole runtime plus incoming replacement under the original 4 GiB CPU contract. Two workers remain the preparation concurrency bound. Regional source hashes must match the original distant specimen. Full source/canonical generation still occurs in each worker; fine hierarchy compilation is restricted before allocation. These changes require fresh forest acceptance; small probes are not a population pass.
